## Where Rewardful is stronger

- **Lower entry price.** Starter is $49 a month, or $490 a year, against $90 a month for Dub's Business plan.
- **No fee by default.** Rewardful states a 0% transaction fee; its optional Managed Payouts cost 3%. Dub charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH and 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise).
- **Unlimited affiliates on every plan.** Dub's Business plan holds 500 partners.
- **Paddle Classic next to Stripe.** Rewardful is a Premier Stripe Partner with two-way Stripe sync.
- **PayPal mass payouts.** Dub offers PayPal payouts in 14 countries.

The limits to know: Starter covers up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue, with 1 campaign and 2 team members. Growth is $99 for up to $15,000, and it is where the branded portal, custom domain and private campaigns start.

## Where Dub Partners is stronger

- **Payouts and tax forms on the entry plan.** Business sends partner payouts for you, with the fee above, and Dub says it handles W-9, W-8 and 1099 forms. On Rewardful, payouts and tax info go through Managed Payouts at 3%.
- **An MCP server on Business**, next to the REST API. Rewardful has a REST API on every plan; its MCP server covers its developer docs.
- **Importers.** Dub imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. Rewardful's support team imports affiliate accounts from a file you send.
- **More seats.** Business includes 10 users, against 2 team members on Rewardful Starter.
- **Links, analytics and payouts in one product**, since Dub Partners runs on Dub's link platform.
- **A marketplace listing and partner discovery**, on Enterprise. Rewardful lists neither in its plan table.

The limits to know: Business covers $2,500 a month in partner payouts, and when you hit your plan's payout limit you can't send more payouts until the next billing period. Advanced is $300 a month with up to $15,000.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose Rewardful when your affiliates are already lined up, you bill through Stripe or Paddle Classic, and you want a known bill: $49 a month for up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue, then $99 for up to $15,000. Choose Dub Partners when you want Dub to send payouts and handle tax forms, you already use Dub for links, or you are moving from one of the tools it imports from; budget $90 a month plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH, and $300 a month on Advanced once payouts pass $2,500 a month. Both offer a 14-day trial and let you cancel any time.

## FAQ

### How do Rewardful and Dub prices compare?

On the entry plans, Rewardful costs less. Rewardful Starter is $49 a month with a 0% transaction fee and covers up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue. Dub Partners Business is $90 a month, covers up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts, and charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH or 8% by card. Rewardful's optional Managed Payouts cost 3%. Prices checked October 12, 2026.

### Can I move from Rewardful to Dub?

Yes. Dub Partners imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. Moving the other way, Rewardful's support team imports affiliate accounts from a file you send them.

### Does Dub Partners handle tax forms?

Yes. Dub says its Business plan handles tax compliance, so you don't send W-9, 1099 or W-8 forms yourself. On Rewardful, tax info is collected through Managed Payouts, which cost 3% (as of October 12, 2026).

### Which one has an MCP server?

Dub Partners lists a REST API and an MCP server on its Business plan. Rewardful has a REST API on every plan, and its MCP server covers its developer docs (as of October 12, 2026).

### Is there an option with no monthly fee?

Affitor, which we make, has no subscription: it costs nothing until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales, with no cap on affiliate revenue. It lists every program on its marketplace and reads Stripe sales with no code. It has no importer, so partners get new links, and it pays partners by bank transfer or Wise rather than PayPal (as of October 12, 2026).
