Pick [Dub Partners](https://dub.co/pricing) if you want Dub to send partner payouts and handle tax forms on its entry plan, at $90 a month plus 5% of the payouts you fund by ACH. Both checked on October 17, 2026.

## Pricing

Rewardful Starter is $49 a month, or $490 a year, and covers up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue. Dub Partners Business is $90 a month and covers up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts. Rewardful states a 0% transaction fee; its optional Managed Payouts cost 3%. Dub charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH and 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise).

The two caps measure different things: Rewardful's counts affiliate revenue, Dub's counts the payouts you send partners. Rewardful Growth is $99 a month for up to $15,000. Dub Advanced is $300 a month for up to $15,000 in payouts, and when you hit your plan's payout limit you can't send more payouts until the next billing period. Both offer a 14-day trial and let you cancel any time.

## Partners

- **Rewardful** has unlimited affiliates on every plan. Starter includes 1 campaign and 2 team members; Growth is where the branded portal, custom domain and private campaigns start.
- **Dub Partners** Business holds 500 partners and 10 users. A marketplace listing and partner discovery come with Enterprise. Rewardful lists neither in its plan table.

## Payouts

- **Rewardful** pays affiliates through Managed Payouts at 3%, which also collects tax info. It offers PayPal mass payouts.
- **Dub Partners** sends partner payouts for you on Business, for the fee above, and Dub says it handles W-9, W-8 and 1099 forms. It offers PayPal payouts in 14 countries.

## Tracking and setup

Both read sales from Stripe natively and credit sales to coupon codes: Rewardful on every plan, Dub through Stripe promotion codes.

- **Rewardful** is a Premier Stripe Partner with two-way Stripe sync, and also supports Paddle Classic. It has a REST API on every plan; its MCP server covers its developer docs. Its support team imports affiliate accounts from a file you send.
- **Dub Partners** supports Stripe and Shopify, and other billing through its API. It lists a REST API and an MCP server on Business, and imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. Links, analytics and payouts sit in one product, since Dub Partners runs on Dub's link platform.

## Which one should you pick?

**Rewardful** if you want the lowest entry price and no fee on sales: your affiliates are already lined up, you bill through Stripe or Paddle Classic, and you want a known bill of $49 a month for up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue. **Dub Partners** if you want payouts and tax forms handled on the entry plan, you already use Dub for links, or you are moving from one of the tools it imports from; budget $90 a month plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH.

## FAQ

<Faq>

### How do Rewardful and Dub prices compare?

On the entry plans, Rewardful costs less. Rewardful Starter is $49 a month with a 0% transaction fee and covers up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue. Dub Partners Business is $90 a month, covers up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts, and charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH or 8% by card. Rewardful's optional Managed Payouts cost 3%. Prices checked October 17, 2026.

### Can I move from Rewardful to Dub?

Yes. Dub Partners imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. Moving the other way, Rewardful's support team imports affiliate accounts from a file you send them.

### Does Dub Partners handle tax forms?

Yes. Dub says its Business plan handles tax compliance, so you don't send W-9, 1099 or W-8 forms yourself. On Rewardful, tax info is collected through Managed Payouts, which cost 3% (as of October 17, 2026).

### Which one has an MCP server?

Dub Partners lists a REST API and an MCP server on its Business plan. Rewardful has a REST API on every plan, and its MCP server covers its developer docs (as of October 17, 2026).

### Is there an option with no monthly fee?

Affitor, which we make, has no subscription: it costs nothing until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales, with no cap on affiliate revenue. It lists every program on its marketplace and reads Stripe sales with no code. It has no importer, so partners get new links, and it pays partners by bank transfer or Wise rather than PayPal (as of October 17, 2026).
