## Quick answer: Rewardful or FirstPromoter?

Rewardful is the better pick for most Stripe SaaS in 2026: it states a 0% transaction fee plainly, gives 50% more affiliate-revenue headroom at the entry price, and has no affiliate limit on any plan. Pick FirstPromoter if you bill through Chargebee, Recurly, or Braintree, or if you want multi-tier commissions and tax-form collection on every plan. Both include an API on the $49 plan, and attribution is a tie. If you want to skip the subscription entirely, Affitor charges $0 until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates.

## Rewardful vs FirstPromoter pricing: same floor, different ceilings

Both ladders look identical from a distance: $49, then $99, then $149 and up. The differences live in what each rung caps and what it unlocks. Both vendors meter you by monthly *affiliate revenue*, meaning the revenue your affiliates generate, not your total revenue.

**Rewardful** prices like this ([rewardful.com/pricing](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing), checked Oct 2026):

- Starter, $49/mo: up to $7,500/mo in affiliate-attributed revenue, 1 campaign, up to 2 team members
- Growth, $99/mo: up to $15,000/mo, unlimited campaigns and team members, branded affiliate portal, custom domain
- Enterprise, $149+/mo: up to $30,000/mo on the entry Enterprise tier, phone support, one-click PayPal payouts

Rewardful states a 0% transaction fee on every plan, plainly, on the pricing page; its optional Managed Payouts cost 3% of payouts. There is a 14-day trial, and annual billing gives 12 months for the price of 10, so Growth runs $990 a year paid annually instead of $1,188 paid month to month. There is no $0 plan.

**FirstPromoter** uses the same three rungs with different limits ([firstpromoter.com/pricing](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing), checked Oct 2026):

- Starter, $49/mo: up to $5,000/mo in affiliate revenue, 3 campaigns, up to 1,000 affiliates
- Business, $99/mo: up to $15,000/mo, unlimited campaigns and affiliates, 2 websites, custom dashboard domain, one-click PayPal payouts
- Enterprise, from $149/mo: above $15,000/mo, 3 websites, managed auto payouts, white-labeling

The trial is 14 days with no credit card required, there is no $0 plan, and team members are unlimited on every plan. FirstPromoter's pricing page lists no transaction fee; Rewardful's states one. (Rewardful's page does not say whether its trial needs a card, which is why we make that claim for FirstPromoter alone.)

Look past the headline price and the entry plans ration different things. Rewardful's Starter allows 1 campaign and up to 2 team members but gives you more revenue headroom and unlimited affiliates. FirstPromoter's Starter allows 3 campaigns (promoter groups with their own commission terms), unlimited team members, and up to 1,000 affiliates, but caps revenue lower and cannot run a second website. Which constraint bites first depends on whether your bottleneck is people, programs, or sales volume.

Two honest observations. First, FirstPromoter's $5,000/mo entry cap is the lowest in its peer group, so upgrade pressure arrives earliest: if your affiliates drive $6,000 in sales next month, you are over FirstPromoter's Starter cap and shopping for the $99 plan, while on Rewardful you are still $1,500 under the limit. Second, both ladders meter success. The better your program performs, the sooner you pay more, whether or not your margins moved.

**Verdict: Rewardful leads on pricing.** 50% more affiliate-revenue headroom at the same $49, unlimited affiliates, and a transaction-fee policy stated in writing.

## Attribution: two cookie models, one blind spot

This criterion is closer to a tie than either vendor's marketing suggests.

**Rewardful** attribution is cookie-based with a [60-day default window](https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/2155812-how-long-are-referral-cookies-valid) you can change per campaign, and you can choose first-touch or last-touch credit. That choice matters more than it sounds: first-touch pays the affiliate whose link the buyer clicked first, which protects reviewers and content sites; last-touch pays the most recent click, which tends to favor whoever the buyer touched on the way to checkout.

**FirstPromoter** is also cookie-based, with first-click or last-click credit and a cookie life you can set up to 9,999 days.

A 60-day window is generous: a click today still credits the affiliate if the customer converts within two months. For the common case, one browser, one device, a conversion inside the window, both systems work.

The shared weakness is that cookie-based attribution breaks on cookie loss, and the failure is silent. The buyer switches devices, clears cookies, or converts after the window closes; the affiliate loses credit, and no dashboard shows you the miss. Neither vendor is worse than the other here. It is the same model with the same blind spot, so do not let either compare page convince you tracking is the reason to switch between these two.

**Verdict: a tie.** The same cookie model with the same silent failure mode; tracking is not the reason to pick either.

## API and integrations: an API on both, broader billing on one

**Rewardful** includes its REST API on every plan, including the $49 Starter. Its billing connections are Stripe (it is a Premier Stripe Partner) and Paddle Classic; its help center says it does not support Paddle Billing. Setup is two scripts or a one-click integration.

**FirstPromoter** connects natively to five billing systems (Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee, Recurly, Braintree) and lists its Admin, Affiliate and Tracking APIs and webhooks on every plan, including the $49 Starter. The July 2026 version of this page said its API started at $99; FirstPromoter's pricing page in October 2026 shows it on every plan.

That leaves one clean decision rule: if you bill through a provider Rewardful does not support, Rewardful is off the table and FirstPromoter is not. Billing fit overrides every other criterion on this page, because a tracker that cannot see your invoices tracks nothing.

Worth knowing if you automate with AI agents: FirstPromoter lists an MCP server in beta on every plan, while Rewardful's MCP server covers its developer documentation.

**Verdict: FirstPromoter leads on integrations.** Both ship an API at $49, and FirstPromoter adds three more native billing connections and an MCP server in beta. If you bill through Stripe, this criterion is close to a tie.

## Payouts and tax: two different back offices

**FirstPromoter** has the deeper commission and tax engine. Recurring, lifetime, and multi-tier commissions (up to 3 levels), W-9 and W-8BEN collection in the affiliate portal, fraud detection, and mass payouts by PayPal, Wise, Stripe, crypto, or bank transfer are on every plan. One-click direct PayPal payouts arrive on Business, and managed auto payouts, where FirstPromoter pays affiliates for you, are on Enterprise.

**Rewardful** keeps this surface smaller but makes hands-off payouts available earlier. PayPal mass payouts and Wise bulk payouts are on every plan, and optional Managed Payouts, where Rewardful collects tax info, distributes funds, and handles compliance, are available on every plan for a 3% processing fee. One-click PayPal payouts are on Enterprise.

For a program with more than a handful of affiliates, the tax-form question is not cosmetic: someone has to collect the paperwork before payouts go out, and doing it in a spreadsheet is exactly the kind of chore that stops happening by month three.

One caution while you research: FirstPromoter's own compare page knocks Rewardful on features. Those claims come from a competitor's marketing page, so weigh them accordingly, and apply the same skepticism to the Affitor section below.

**Verdict: a split.** FirstPromoter leads on tax paperwork and commission rules, with forms collected on every plan at no extra fee; Rewardful leads on hands-off payouts, with Managed Payouts on every plan at 3%.

## Who should pick which

**Pick Rewardful if** you bill through Stripe, want more headroom at $49 and no affiliate limit, and value a transaction fee that is stated as 0% rather than left unsaid. Its Starter cap gives you 50% more affiliate-revenue headroom than FirstPromoter's at the same price, and Managed Payouts let it pay affiliates for you on any plan.

**Pick FirstPromoter if** you bill through Chargebee or another provider beyond Rewardful's Stripe and Paddle Classic, want multi-tier or detailed commission rules, or want W-9 and W-8BEN forms collected inside the product instead of in a spreadsheet. It also gives you 3 campaigns and unlimited team members at $49. Expect to outgrow the $5,000/mo Starter cap first.

Neither choice is a mistake at this price. Both are mature products aimed squarely at subscription SaaS, and for many teams the deciding factor is simply which billing provider they already use.
