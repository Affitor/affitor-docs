## Quick answer: Tolt or Rewardful?

Rewardful is the better pick for most Stripe SaaS starting an affiliate program in 2026: it costs $20 less at entry, states a 0% transaction fee, and lets you hand payouts to it on any plan through Managed Payouts. Pick Tolt when payouts and program count decide it: auto payouts to PayPal, Wise, local bank, crypto, or wire from the $99 plan, 2 programs on the entry plan, and a third more revenue headroom at $99. Both publish an API. If you want to skip the subscription entirely, Affitor charges $0 until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates.

## Tolt vs Rewardful pricing: $20 apart at the door, different math at $99

Both products sell a flat monthly subscription metered by *affiliate revenue*: the revenue your affiliates generate each month, not your total revenue. The ladders look similar from a distance; the differences live in the caps and the fine print.

**Tolt** prices like this (per [tolt.com/pricing](https://tolt.com/pricing), checked October 2026):

| Plan | Price | Affiliate revenue cap | Programs | Payouts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $69/mo | $10,000/mo | 2 | Manual |
| Growth | $99/mo | $20,000/mo | 5 | Automated, 2% processing fee |
| Pro | $199/mo | $50,000/mo | Unlimited | Automated, 2% processing fee |
| Enterprise | Custom | $50,001+/mo | Unlimited | Automated, 2% processing fee |

Affiliates and referrals are unlimited on every plan. The trial is 14 days with no credit card required, and there is a 30-day refund policy. There is no $0 plan.

**Rewardful** uses the familiar three rungs (per [rewardful.com/pricing](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing), checked October 2026):

| Plan | Price | Affiliate revenue cap | Notable limits and unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49/mo | $7,500/mo | 1 campaign, up to 2 team members |
| Growth | $99/mo | $15,000/mo | Unlimited campaigns and team, branded portal, custom domain |
| Enterprise | $149+/mo | Up to $30,000/mo on the entry tier | Phone support, one-click PayPal payouts |

Rewardful states a 0% transaction fee on every plan, plainly, on the pricing page. Affiliates are unlimited on every plan. There is a 14-day trial (the page does not say whether a card is required, so we will not claim it either way), annual billing gives 12 months for the price of 10, and there is no $0 plan. The REST API is included on every plan.

Now the head-to-head math. On the entry rung, Rewardful is $20 less in absolute terms; Tolt answers with a higher revenue cap ($10,000/mo against $7,500/mo) and 2 programs and 3 team members where Rewardful's Starter allows 1 campaign and 2 team members. At the $99 rung, the value flips direction: Tolt's Growth caps at $20,000/mo where Rewardful's caps at $15,000/mo, a third more headroom for the same money.

Then there is the fee print. Both say 0%, and both charge if you want the tool to pay partners for you. Tolt's auto payouts carry a 2% processing fee and start on Growth; Basic payouts are manual. Rewardful's optional Managed Payouts carry a 3% processing fee and are available on every plan. Without them, you send the money yourself: Rewardful lists PayPal mass payouts and Wise bulk payouts on every plan, and Tolt's FAQ says you can pay affiliates with PayPal or Wise.

And note that both ladders meter success: the better your affiliates perform, the sooner you cross a cap and get the upgrade email, whether or not your margins moved. If you want the cost picture across the whole category, [the affiliate software pricing comparison](/blog/best-affiliate-software-saas) lays out the plans side by side.

**Verdict: Rewardful leads on pricing, narrowly.** A $20-lower floor and 12 months for the price of 10 on annual billing. But if you expect to live at the $99 plan, Tolt's extra $5,000/month of headroom is the better buy, and its payout automation costs 2% against Rewardful's 3%.

## Attribution: both start from a cookie

This criterion is closer to a tie than either vendor's positioning suggests.

**Rewardful** uses a cookie with a [60-day default window](https://help.rewardful.com/en/articles/2155812-how-long-are-referral-cookies-valid) you can change per campaign, and lets you choose first-touch or last-touch credit. That choice matters more than it sounds: first-touch pays the affiliate whose link the buyer clicked first, which protects reviewers and content sites; last-touch pays the most recent click, which favors whoever touched the buyer on the way to checkout.

**Tolt** also starts from a cookie. Per its [JavaScript docs](https://docs.tolt.com/javascript/overview), a visit through a partner link stores the click with a cookie duration, and a `signup` call turns that click into a lead tied to your customer's email or ID.

For the common case, one browser, one device, a conversion inside the window, both systems work fine. The shared weakness is that the cookie step breaks on cookie loss, and the failure is silent. The buyer switches from laptop to phone, clears cookies, runs a blocker, or converts after the window closes; the affiliate loses credit, and no dashboard shows you the miss. Neither vendor is worse than the other here on the public record, so do not let a compare page convince you tracking is the reason to switch between these two.

**Verdict: a tie.** Both start from a cookie with the same silent failure mode; attribution is not the reason to pick either.

## API and integrations: both ship an API

**Rewardful** includes its REST API on every plan, including the $49 Starter. It is a Premier Stripe Partner, setup is two scripts or a one-click integration, and its billing connections are Stripe and Paddle Classic; its help center says it does not support Paddle Billing.

**Tolt** documents a [REST API](https://docs.tolt.com/introduction) for partners, customers, links, clicks, commissions, and transactions, plus webhooks. It integrates with Stripe, Paddle (its help center covers both [Paddle Billing](https://help.tolt.com/en/articles/8455444-how-to-set-up-paddle-billing-with-tolt) and Paddle Classic), and Chargebee. The July 2026 version of this page said no API was surfaced on Tolt's pricing page; Tolt's docs show one.

Worth knowing if you automate with AI agents: Tolt has an [MCP connection to Claude](https://help.tolt.com/en/articles/14321072-integrate-tolt-mcp-with-claude), while Rewardful's MCP server covers its developer documentation.

**Verdict: Tolt leads narrowly on integrations.** Both publish an API; Tolt adds Chargebee and Paddle Billing and a live MCP connection for agents. If you bill through Stripe, this criterion is close to a tie.

## Payouts and program limits: global rails vs managed payouts

**Tolt** pays affiliates through PayPal, Wise, local bank transfer, crypto, or wire, with auto payouts from the $99 Growth plan up (that automation is where the 2% processing fee applies). Growth also collects W-9/W-8 forms and files 1099s. Affiliates and referrals are unlimited on every plan, and the ladder scales from 2 programs on Basic to 5 on Growth to unlimited on Pro, which also adds a dedicated Slack channel.

**Rewardful** keeps this surface smaller but opens it earlier. PayPal mass payouts and Wise bulk payouts are on every plan, and optional Managed Payouts, where Rewardful collects tax info, distributes funds, and handles compliance, are available on every plan at 3%. One-click PayPal payouts are on Enterprise. On the program side, Starter allows 1 campaign and up to 2 team members at $49.

For a program with more than a handful of affiliates, payout mechanics are not cosmetic. Someone has to send the money every month, and doing it by hand across PayPal and bank transfers is exactly the kind of chore that stops happening by month three. If your partners are international, Tolt's auto payouts cover local bank, crypto, and wire as well as PayPal and Wise.

**Verdict: Tolt leads on payouts and program limits.** Five payout rails with automation from $99 at 2%, tax forms and 1099 filing from Growth, and more programs per plan. Rewardful's Managed Payouts are the simpler hands-off option on its $49 plan, at 3%.

## When to choose Tolt over Rewardful

- **Your affiliates are global and you refuse to run payouts by hand.** Auto payouts to PayPal, Wise, local bank, crypto, or wire from $99/mo, at 2%, is the clearest payout advantage Tolt has.
- **You expect to live at the $99 plan.** $20,000/mo of affiliate-revenue headroom against Rewardful's $15,000/mo means your affiliates can grow a third further before the next upgrade email.
- **You want more than one program on the entry plan.** Tolt Basic allows 2 programs and 3 team members, against Rewardful Starter's 1 campaign and 2 team members.
- **You want an exit ramp.** A 14-day no-card trial plus a 30-day refund is the friendlier try-it policy of the two; Rewardful's page offers 14 days and stays silent on the card.
