## Which affiliate software has no monthly fee?

As of October 12, 2026, Affitor has no monthly fee: $0 until a program makes $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of each affiliate sale. Lemon Squeezy has none either but takes a fee on every sale, plus 3% on affiliate-referred ones. Endorsely and PromoteKit have capped free plans; others start with a trial, a plan or a quote.

We build Affitor, so weigh this page with that in mind. Every price below comes from the vendor's own pricing page, checked in October 2026, not from third-party lists. Every tool has a catch, ours included. The map places each tool by whether it charges a fixed monthly fee and by whether it brings you partners.

## Four ways to start at $0, and the catch in each tool

The tools that cost $0 at the start mean different things by it.

**$0 until a total.** Affitor charges no platform fee until a program's affiliate revenue reaches $10,000, counted all-time and per program, then 3.5% of each sale. The counter never resets and never expires, so a slow program pays no fee for as long as it takes.

**$0 under a usage cap.** Endorsely's cap is monthly revenue: free while affiliate revenue stays under $1,000 a month, so one strong month moves you to a paid plan. PromoteKit's cap is a referral count: your first 3 referrals in total, then $29 a month. Both are good for a test.

**No monthly fee, a cut from the first sale.** Lemon Squeezy adds 3% to each affiliate-referred sale on top of its base fee of 5% + 50¢ per sale, plus 0.5% on subscription payments. It fits if Lemon Squeezy already runs your checkout.

**A trial, not a free plan.** Rewardful, FirstPromoter and Trackdesk are mature tools with a fixed bill from the end of a 14-day trial.

The catch in each one, from each vendor's pricing page, checked October 12, 2026:

**[Affitor](https://affitor.com/pricing).** $0, with no subscription and no setup fee, until a program passes $10,000 in affiliate revenue (all-time, per program), then 3.5% of each affiliate sale. The 3.5% has no cap, so the fee grows with affiliate revenue. No importer: partners get new links. Payouts go by bank transfer or Wise, not PayPal.

**[Endorsely](https://www.endorsely.com/pricing).** $0 on Starter, up to $1,000 a month in affiliate revenue, then $39 a month on Pro (up to $5,000 a month) and $99 a month up to $20,000. The cap is monthly: after $1,000 in monthly affiliate sales, you upgrade to keep tracking. Payouts are a PayPal bulk file you send yourself.

**[PromoteKit](https://www.promotekit.com/pricing).** $0 for your first 3 referrals, then $29 a month on Pro, up to $10,000 a month; above that, contact sales. 3 referrals is a trial in practice. Stripe is required. Optional payouts to affiliates' bank accounts add a [fee on top of the payout total](https://www.promotekit.com/docs/payouts/stripe-payouts#fees): 5% if you fund them from a bank account (ACH), 8% by credit or debit card.

**[Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing).** No monthly fee. Base fee of 5% + 50¢ per sale, as merchant of record, plus 3% on each affiliate-referred sale, from the first one. [Its fees page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees) adds 0.5% on subscription payments and 1.5% each on international and PayPal payments. Tracks sales made through its own checkout. Affiliates pay 2% on their payouts.

**[Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing).** 14-day trial, then $49 a month on Starter (up to $7,500 a month) and $99 on Growth (up to $15,000). A trial, not a free plan: $49 a month whether or not affiliates sell. Optional Managed Payouts cost 3%.

**[FirstPromoter](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing).** 14-day trial, no card, then $49 a month on Starter (up to $5,000 a month) and $99 on Business (up to $15,000). [Its own site](https://firstpromoter.com/solutions/free-affiliate-marketing-software) says "There is no permanent free plan." Starter allows up to 1,000 affiliates.

**[Trackdesk](https://trackdesk.com/pricing).** 14-day trial, then $299 a month on Business and $499 on Enterprise. Some articles still describe a free plan. [Its help center](https://help.trackdesk.com/en/articles/11405479-limits-of-the-deprecated-free-plan) says the Free plan is no longer available to new users; existing users keep it.

The commissions you pay affiliates come on top with every tool.

## Which one fits a SaaS under $1k MRR

Below $1,000 in MRR, affiliate revenue is small and uncertain, so pick a tool that costs $0 while you find out whether affiliates work for you.

**Affitor** if you'd rather not watch a monthly cap: there is no platform fee on the first $10,000 in affiliate revenue, however long it takes, and every feature is included from day one.

**Endorsely** if you want a fixed plan later: free under $1,000 a month, then $39.

**PromoteKit** if you sell through Stripe and want to try a few referrals before paying $29 a month.

**Lemon Squeezy** if it already runs your checkout, since its affiliate tools need no extra product.

## What Affitor's $0 covers, and where it falls short

The $0 covers the whole product: every feature, nothing per partner or per seat, a public marketplace listing for your program, Stripe connected through OAuth with no code, and a REST API and SDK for other billing systems. Full details are on the [pricing page](https://affitor.com/pricing). Beyond the software, the Affitor team helps you recruit, onboard and train partners: [affitor.com/join](https://affitor.com/join).

Where it falls short, as of October 2026:

**No importer.** You invite partners by email or CSV, 50 at a time, and they get new links. Past sales and unpaid commissions stay in your old tool.

**Payouts by bank transfer or Wise.** Withdrawals go out after a manual review. PayPal isn't offered.

**Stripe is the native integration.** Paddle, Lemon Squeezy and other billing systems send sales through the API or SDK.

**No bulk export yet.** There is no one-click export of partners, clicks or commissions (details in the FAQ below).

**No cap on the 3.5%.** The fee keeps growing with affiliate revenue.
