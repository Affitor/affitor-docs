## Four kinds of "free"

The tools that cost $0 at the start mean different things by it, and the difference decides what you pay in month six.

- **Free until a total.** Affitor is $0 until a program's affiliate revenue reaches $10,000, counted all-time and per program, then 3.5% of each sale. The counter never resets and never expires, so a slow program stays at $0 for as long as it takes.
- **Free under a monthly cap.** Endorsely is free under $1,000 a month in affiliate revenue, and PromoteKit for 3 referrals. Both are good for a test. One strong month moves you to a paid plan.
- **No monthly fee, a cut from the first sale.** Lemon Squeezy adds 3% to each affiliate-referred sale on top of its 5% + 50¢ per sale. It fits if Lemon Squeezy already runs your checkout.
- **A trial, not a free plan.** Rewardful, FirstPromoter and Trackdesk are mature tools with a fixed bill from the end of a 14-day trial.

- **Affitor** if you'd rather not watch a monthly cap: the first $10,000 in affiliate revenue is free however long it takes, and every feature is included from day one.
- **Endorsely** if you want a fixed plan later: free under $1,000 a month, then $39.

## What Affitor's $0 covers, and where it falls short

The $0 covers the whole product: every feature, nothing per partner or per seat, a public marketplace listing for your program, Stripe connected through OAuth with no code, and a REST API and SDK for other billing systems. Full details are on the [pricing page](https://affitor.com/pricing).

Where it falls short, as of October 2026:

- **No importer.** You invite partners by email or CSV, 50 at a time, and they get new links. Past sales and unpaid commissions stay in your old tool.
- **Payouts by bank transfer or Wise.** Withdrawals go out after a manual review. PayPal isn't offered.
- **Stripe is the native integration.** Paddle, Lemon Squeezy and other billing systems send sales through the API or SDK.
- **No bulk export yet.** There is no one-click export of partners, clicks or commissions (details in the FAQ below).
- **No cap on the 3.5%.** At high affiliate revenue, a flat plan costs less.
