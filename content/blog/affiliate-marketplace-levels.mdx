From November 27, every program on the Affitor Marketplace sits at one of three levels: Live, Launch or Verified. Your level decides where partners find your program and what they see next to its name.

## What is the Affitor Marketplace?

**For brands,** the Affitor Marketplace is where your affiliate program shows up. Each program gets its own page with your product, your commission and your terms, next to the other SaaS and AI programs partners are already browsing.

**For partners,** from affiliates and creators to AI agents, it is where they find programs to promote: they browse the list, filter it, apply on the program page and get their link once approved.

Partners come to the marketplace on their own, so the easier your program is to see, the more partners apply. The three levels below decide that.

## How are Live, Launch and Verified different?

| Level | What your brand needs | What partners see |
|---|---|---|

| **Launch** | • Your program is Live<br />• Payments: Stripe accepting payments, or at least one real paid sale from your server<br />• A complete program page: business name, website, logo and an About of 40 characters or more<br />• Terms: a commission, an attribution window of 1 to 365 days and a hold period of 0 to 31 days | • Your program in the default list, the one partners see when they open the marketplace<br />• No *Pre-launch* label on your card<br />• Your program page, where partners apply and get their link |
| **Live** | • Business: a business name and a website<br />• Commission: a sale commission saved<br />• Sales: Stripe connected, or one sale sent from your server<br />• You pressed **Go live** | • Your program page: partners can apply and get their link<br />• Your program shows only when partners turn on *All programs*<br />• A *Pre-launch* label on your card |

The higher your level, the more partners see your program and the more they trust it. At Verified, your program carries a blue check mark: partners know it is a real program with real payments, real activity and no unpaid invoices, so they are willing to put work into promoting it. At Launch, your program sits in the default list, where most partners start looking, so more of them see it and apply. At Live, your program is on the marketplace, but only partners who turn on a filter will find it.

No level costs extra and none needs an application. Your program's real data sets its level, Affitor checks it again on its own, and the fee stays [$0 until your first $10,000 through affiliates](https://affitor.com/pricing).

## What do you need for each level?

### Live

A program goes live after four setup steps: Business, Commission, Tracking and Go live. The Get started list on your dashboard shows how many are done, and Verified comes after Live.

<img src="/blog/shots/marketplace-levels-get-started.png" alt="Affitor dashboard with the Get started list: Business, Commission and Tracking done, Go live next, and Verified after Live" />

*The Get started list: four setup steps, then Verified after Live. Before you go live, the dashboard only shows the test click, lead and sale from Check it works.*

On the Go live step, three rows must be done before you can go live:

* **Business:** a business name and a website. The logo is optional at this step.
* **Commission:** save a sale commission and choose how new partners join.
* **Sales:** connect Stripe, or send one sale from your server. A test sale counts here, so you can go live before your first real customer.
* **Clicks** are optional to go live, but install the tracker before partners start sending traffic.

With the three required rows done, select **Go live**.

<img src="/blog/shots/marketplace-levels-go-live.png" alt="Go live step: Business and Commission done, Clicks optional, Sales still to fix, Go live button not yet active" />

*The Go live step: Go live turns on once Business, Commission and Sales are done.*

### Launch

To move from Live to Launch, your program needs three things:

1. **Real payments.** Stripe is connected on the program and your Stripe account can accept payments, or your server sends sales to Affitor and at least one real paid sale has come in. Test sales don't count at this level, including a test sale you send from `Settings › Tracking`. A purchase by your team, or by a partner for themselves, doesn't count either.
2. **A complete program page.**
   * Business name
   * Website: an http:// or https:// address
   * Logo: one you uploaded, or one Affitor finds on your website
   * About: at least 40 characters about your product
3. **Terms.**
   * A commission on sales: a type and a rate
   * An attribution window of 1 to 365 days
   * A hold period of 0 to 31 days
   * New programs start at 60 and 15 days, which already meet these terms

Once all three hold, Affitor adds your program to the default list. There is nothing to request.

**Still showing Pre-launch?** It is usually one of these:

* Only test sales, or sales made by your own team.
* Stripe still asks you to finish setting up your account.
* No logo, or an About under 40 characters.

To check, open the marketplace, turn on *All programs* and find your card. Where each condition lives in your dashboard is in [How marketplace levels work](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/marketplace-levels).

### Verified

There is nothing to apply for. The blue check mark shows while all six conditions hold, and goes away by itself when one stops:

1. Your program is live and its website is a public https:// address.
2. Your brand account is active.
3. Your payments are connected: Stripe on the program, accepting payments, or a real sale your server sent in the last 90 days.
4. Affitor has recorded at least one real click, lead or sale.
5. No Affitor invoice is overdue.
6. Affitor has not removed the badge from your program.

Only programs at the Launch level show the badge. When partners have already been paid through Affitor, the badge also says so. For partners, it is the quickest way to find a verified affiliate program: turn on *Verified only* in the marketplace filter.

## Start your program: set it up in one afternoon

Our launch guide estimates 20 to 30 minutes to set up a program. Tracking and reaching your partners take a little longer. Here is the full path, in order.

## Step 1: Create your program on Affitor

[Create a free account](https://affitor.com/signup?utm_source=affitor_blog&utm_medium=cta&utm_campaign=affiliate-marketplace-levels), then set up your program: your business details, the information partners see, your commission (on signups, sales, or subscription payments), and who can join.

New to running a program? Our guide [How to Start an Affiliate Program for Your SaaS](/blog/how-to-start-saas-affiliate-program) covers commission rates and payout terms.

## Step 2: Count your sales

Affitor can count sales in two ways:

* **Connect Stripe.** Go to `Settings › Tracking › Count sales › Connect Stripe` and approve the connection on Stripe's page. You do not create an API key or a webhook ([Stripe payment tracking](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/tracking/payment-tracking-stripe)). Affitor credits each sale and renewal to the partner who sent the customer, and reverses the commission when a sale is refunded.
* **Send sales from your server.** Call the Affitor API after a payment succeeds ([Track Sale API](https://affitor.com/docs/api-reference/track-sale)), and again when you refund one ([Track Refund API](https://affitor.com/docs/api-reference/track-refund)).

Then run **Check it works** under `Settings › Tracking`: Affitor sends a test click, signup and sale through your program, with no real money.

For more on Stripe programs, see [Best Affiliate Tracking Software for Stripe](/blog/stripe-affiliate-program).

## Step 3: Add the tracker to your site

Add the Affitor tracker script to the pages where partner traffic lands, so clicks are recorded ([integration guide](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/tracking/quickstart-integration)). If you use an AI coding agent, you can check the setup with `npx affitor onboard`, which sends a test click, signup and sale.
