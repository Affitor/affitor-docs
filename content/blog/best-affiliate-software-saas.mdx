## Everyone tracks the sale. Most bill you before the first one.

We compared all 19 tools on what decides whether an affiliate program pays off for a SaaS that is just starting one: what you pay before affiliates send a sale, whether there is a cap on affiliate revenue, whether the vendor puts your program in front of affiliates, how partners get paid, and how sales come in from your billing. Nearly all of them track a referral well. They differ on when they start charging and on who finds the affiliates.

**Affitor, which we make, charges nothing until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates and lists every program on a [marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace) affiliates browse.** After the first $10,000 it takes 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales, with no subscription, no seat fee and no cap on affiliate revenue.
