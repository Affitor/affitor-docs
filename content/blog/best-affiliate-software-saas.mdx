The right affiliate software for a SaaS depends less on features than on how it charges you and how much affiliate revenue you have today. All nine tools below do the core job: give each partner a link, match sales from your billing system, and pay commissions. They differ on price shape, revenue caps, fees on top, and whether they help you find affiliates or only track the ones you bring.

We build Affitor, one of the nine, so read this with that in mind. The tools are listed by entry price, lowest first, which puts Affitor at the top; the order is not a ranking. Every price was checked on the vendor's own pricing page on October 12, 2026, and each one links to that page. Where Affitor is the weaker choice, this post says so.

## Quick answer

- **Pay nothing until affiliates bring in revenue:** Affitor. Free until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales.
- **Cheapest flat price on Stripe:** PromoteKit, $29 a month for up to $10,000 a month in affiliate revenue.
- **Simplest Stripe setup at a known monthly cost:** Rewardful, from $49 a month with a 0% transaction fee.
- **Billing outside Stripe (Paddle, Chargebee, Recurly, Braintree):** FirstPromoter, from $49 a month.
- **One flat price with no revenue cap:** Partnero, from $59 a month ($49 billed annually).
- **A flat plan that also handles payouts and 1099s:** Tolt, from $69 a month ($99 for automatic payouts and tax filing).
- **Developer teams that want links, payouts and an API in one product:** Dub Partners, from $90 a month plus a 5% payout fee.
- **Someone to bring you B2B SaaS affiliates and move your program for you:** Reditus, from $99 a month billed annually.
- **Affiliates, resellers and co-sell partners in one enterprise system:** PartnerStack, priced by quote after a demo.

## How we compared them

We read each vendor's own pricing page first, then its help docs, all on October 12, 2026. We didn't use review sites or third-party price lists, because they trail behind price changes. When a vendor doesn't publish something, the tables say "not published" or "not listed" instead of guessing. We don't give scores or stars, because a single score hides the trade-off you actually care about.

Six questions did most of the sorting:

1. **How does it charge?** Flat plan, flat plan with revenue caps, plan plus a fee, or a percentage.
2. **What happens when you grow?** Caps move you to a pricier plan because your affiliates did well.
3. **How is a sale tied to a partner?** A cookie on the click, and whether the customer is locked to the partner at signup.
4. **Does it bring you affiliates?** A marketplace or network, or only the partners you recruit.
5. **How hard is it to switch in?** Importers and migration help.
6. **Can a developer or a coding agent set it up?** API, SDKs, MCP.

This update drops impact.com and Post Affiliate Pro, which serve a wider set of merchants than SaaS, and adds PromoteKit, Partnero and Reditus, which show up in the same searches as the rest.

## Five ways these tools charge you

Most buying mistakes come from comparing tools that charge in different ways as if they were the same product. The nine fall into five groups:

- **Flat plan with revenue caps:** PromoteKit, Rewardful, FirstPromoter, Tolt. Predictable until your affiliates pass the cap, then you move up a plan.
- **Flat plan without a revenue cap:** Partnero. Plans differ by number of programs and seats instead.
- **Plan plus a fee on payouts:** Dub Partners (5% of payouts) and Reditus (5% by card or 2% by invoice on commissions).
- **Percentage of affiliate revenue, no plan:** Affitor. Free on the first $10,000 per program, then 3.5%.
- **Quote:** PartnerStack.

How they track sales, find partners, and handle a switch:

| Tool | Tracking | Brings you affiliates | Switching in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affitor | Cookie on the click, customer tied at signup, sales read from Stripe | Marketplace listing on every program (new and small) | No importer; invite by email or CSV, 50 at a time |
| PromoteKit | Cookie (60-day default) and Stripe promo codes | No | Not listed |
| Rewardful | Cookie, first or last touch, plus coupon codes | No | Not listed |
| FirstPromoter | Cookie up to 9,999 days, first or last click, plus coupons | No | Historical Stripe import |
| Partnero | Links and coupons | Runs a marketplace | Built-in Rewardful import |
| Tolt | Links and coupons | No | Imports customers and sales from your billing system |
| Dub Partners | Cookie (90-day default), customer tied at signup | Partner network on Enterprise only | Importers for six tools (Business and up) |
| Reditus | Stripe coupons and referral IDs | Network of 28,000+ B2B SaaS affiliates (their figure) | Free white-glove migration, old links keep working |
| PartnerStack | Links, or lead and deal registration | Marketplace on every plan | Guided onboarding |

Developer access is close to standard now. Affitor, Rewardful and FirstPromoter publish an API on every plan, PromoteKit on Pro, and Reditus, Partnero and Dub Partners list API and webhooks too. Dub Partners, FirstPromoter (beta), PartnerStack and Affitor (beta) also offer an MCP server or connector for AI assistants. Tolt lists neither on its pricing page.

"Not listed" means we couldn't find it on the vendor's pricing page or docs on October 12, 2026, not that it can't exist.

## The tools, one by one

### Affitor: pay only after affiliates bring revenue

Affitor runs your affiliate program and lists it where affiliates look for programs to promote.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** $0 until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales. No subscription, no setup fee, and nothing per partner or per seat. One weekly invoice covers partner commissions and the fee. [See pricing](https://affitor.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** a first-party cookie on the click (`?aff=`), the customer tied to the partner at signup, and sales read from Stripe after a one-time connect. Other billing systems send sales through the [REST API or SDK](/docs/brand/tracking/tracking-overview).
- **Good:** nothing to pay while you find out whether affiliates work for you. Every program gets a public marketplace listing and an AI-drafted program page. Developers get a REST API, browser and Node SDKs, a [CLI](/docs/brand/cli/commands) that ends setup with a test click, signup and sale, and an [MCP server](/docs/api-reference/mcp) in beta.
- **Watch out:** there's no importer, so moving from another tool means inviting partners by email or CSV, 50 at a time, and partners get new links. Past sales and unpaid commissions don't come with you. Our marketplace is young and much smaller than Reditus's or PartnerStack's networks. Partner withdrawals go out by bank transfer or Wise after a manual review, with no PayPal.

### PromoteKit: cheapest flat plan on Stripe

A lean affiliate tool for businesses that sell through Stripe.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** the free plan covers your first 3 referrals. Pro is $29 a month for up to $10,000 a month in affiliate revenue, with 25% off on yearly billing. Above $10,000 a month it's Enterprise, priced on contact. No transaction fees on any plan. [See pricing](https://www.promotekit.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** links with a cookie (60 days by default, set per campaign) and Stripe promo codes, which need no script on your site.
- **Good:** the lowest flat price here, with unlimited affiliates and team members on every plan, and API and webhooks on Pro.
- **Watch out:** Stripe is required. At 3 referrals the free plan is a trial in practice, and past $10,000 a month you need a quote.

### Rewardful: the simplest Stripe setup at a known price

Built around Stripe, for a SaaS that wants a fixed monthly bill.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** Starter is $49 a month for up to $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue, with 1 campaign and 2 team members. Growth is $99 for up to $15,000. Enterprise starts at $149 for up to $30,000 on its entry tier. The transaction fee is 0%, and optional Managed Payouts cost 3%. 14-day trial; annual billing is 12 months for the price of 10. [See pricing](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** first- or last-touch attribution, plus coupon code tracking.
- **Good:** a Premier Stripe Partner with two-way Stripe sync, a REST API on every plan, and unlimited affiliates.
- **Watch out:** the price doubles at $7,500 a month in affiliate revenue, because your affiliates did well rather than because you used more software. A branded portal, custom domain and private campaigns start on Growth. More in [Rewardful alternatives](/blog/rewardful-alternatives) and [Rewardful vs FirstPromoter](/blog/rewardful-vs-firstpromoter).

### FirstPromoter: billing beyond Stripe and deep commission rules

A flat-fee tool with detailed commission rules and native support for five billing systems.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** Starter is $49 a month for up to $5,000 a month in affiliate revenue, with 3 campaigns and up to 1,000 affiliates. Business is $99 for up to $15,000. Enterprise starts at $149 above that. Yearly plans are $490, $990 and $1,490. Unlimited team members, 14-day trial, no card. [See pricing](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** cookie life up to 9,999 days, first or last click, coupon codes created in Stripe, sub-IDs, and direct URL tracking.
- **Good:** works with Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee, Recurly and Braintree. Multi-tier commissions up to 3 levels, API and webhooks on every plan, an MCP server in beta, and historical Stripe import when you migrate.
- **Watch out:** $5,000 a month is among the lowest caps here. A custom dashboard domain and the in-app embedded dashboard start on Business, and managed payouts are Enterprise only. More in [FirstPromoter alternatives](/blog/firstpromoter-alternatives).

### Partnero: one flat price, no revenue cap

An affiliate, referral and newsletter-referral platform that prices by programs and seats instead of revenue.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** Starter is $59 a month ($49 billed annually). Partner is $199 ($159 annually). Enterprise starts at $599. Every plan has unlimited partners, transactions and revenue. Extra seats are $29 a month each. 30-day trial. [See pricing](https://www.partnero.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** link and coupon tracking.
- **Good:** the price doesn't move as affiliate revenue grows. It has a built-in [Rewardful import](https://help.partnero.com/article/361-migrate-from-rewardful) that uses your Rewardful API key, and no Partnero fee if you pay partners through your own PayPal or Wise.
- **Watch out:** Starter is 1 program and 1 team member, and Partnero-run payouts cost 7%.

### Tolt: payouts and 1099s on a flat plan

A flat-fee tool for SaaS on Stripe or Paddle, with unlimited affiliates on every plan.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** Basic is $69 a month for up to $10,000 a month in affiliate revenue, with 2 programs and manual payouts. Growth is $99 for up to $20,000. Pro is $199 for up to $50,000. Enterprise is on contact. 14-day trial with no card, and a 30-day refund. [See pricing](https://tolt.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** referral links and coupon codes.
- **Good:** unlimited affiliates and referrals on every plan. Growth adds payouts by PayPal, Wise, local bank, crypto and wire, plus W-9/W-8 collection and 1099 filing. At $99 it covers $20,000 a month, more than Rewardful's or FirstPromoter's $99 plans.
- **Watch out:** automatic payouts carry a 2% processing fee, and Basic is manual payouts only. Stripe and Paddle are the supported billing systems. More in [Tolt alternatives](/blog/tolt-alternatives) and [Tolt vs Rewardful](/blog/tolt-vs-rewardful).

### Dub Partners: built for developer teams

Partner programs on top of Dub's link platform, aimed at developer teams.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** Business is $90 a month with up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts. Advanced is $300 with up to $15,000. Both charge a 5% payout fee. Enterprise is custom and annual, with unlimited payouts at 3%. 14-day trial; 10% off yearly. [See pricing](https://dub.co/pricing).
- **Tracking:** the click is captured server-side and stored in a first-party cookie (90 days by default). A lead event at signup ties the customer to the partner, and later sales follow that customer. Last click by default, first click optional.
- **Good:** links, analytics and partner payouts in one product. A REST API, an MCP server and real-time webhooks on Business, plus importers for Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy.
- **Watch out:** the 5% fee sits on top of the plan. When you hit your plan's payout limit you can't send more payouts until the next billing period. The partner network and marketplace listing are Enterprise only.

### Reditus: affiliates brought to you, migration done for you

A B2B SaaS affiliate platform that sells recruiting help as much as tracking.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** Growth is $149 a month, or $99 a month billed annually, up to $60K ARR. Scale Up is $399 a month, annual only, up to $360K ARR. Enterprise starts at $799 a month, annual. Payouts are automated on every plan, with a 5% fee if you pay by card or 2% by invoice, on top of commissions. 14-day trial; no free plan. [See pricing](https://getreditus.com/pricing).
- **Tracking:** Stripe coupon tracking and ID-based referral tracking. Works with Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee, or any processor through its API.
- **Good:** a network it puts at 28,000+ B2B SaaS affiliates, and free white-glove migration on every plan that keeps your old tracking parameters, so partners' existing links still work.
- **Watch out:** a marketplace listing needs annual Scale Up ($4,788 a year). Plans are capped by ARR, and the payout fee applies to every payout.

### PartnerStack: affiliates, resellers and co-sell in one system

A partner relationship platform for B2B companies running more than an affiliate program.

- **Pricing (Oct 12, 2026):** no published price. Launch, Growth and Enterprise are quoted after a demo, based on program size, features and support. [See plans](https://www.partnerstack.com/pricing).
- **What you get:** on Launch, affiliate link tracking or lead and deal registration (you pick one), PartnerStack Marketplace access, partner payments, onboarding and training tools, CRM integrations, and an MCP connector. Growth adds a learning management system and MDF management.
- **Good:** built for B2B partner programs beyond affiliates, with unlimited partners and internal users.
- **Watch out:** you see a price only after a demo, and its own FAQ says most teams are up and running "in weeks". More in [PartnerStack alternatives](/blog/partnerstack-alternatives) and [PartnerStack vs Rewardful](/blog/partnerstack-vs-rewardful).

## Which one should you pick?

**No affiliate revenue yet, or not sure affiliates will work for you:** Affitor, because you pay nothing until a program has made $10,000. If you'd rather have a fixed bill and you're on Stripe, PromoteKit at $29 is the cheapest flat plan.

**You'd rather have a fixed monthly bill:** a flat plan. Rewardful for the simplest Stripe setup, FirstPromoter if you bill through Paddle, Chargebee, Recurly or Braintree, Tolt if you want payouts and 1099s handled, and Partnero if you want a price that never moves with revenue.

**You want affiliates found for you:** Reditus for B2B SaaS. PartnerStack if you also run resellers or co-sell partners and have the budget for a sales-led contract.

**Your team ships everything through code:** Dub Partners if the 5% payout fee is fine with you. Affitor if you'd rather pay on revenue and want setup that ends with a verified test sale.

**You're moving from another tool:** Reditus will do the move for you and keep old links working, and Dub Partners and Partnero have importers. Affitor can run next to your current tool, but today you move partners over yourself.

If paying nothing until affiliates bring in $10,000 fits where you are, start there. [Launch your program free](https://affitor.com/welcome).
