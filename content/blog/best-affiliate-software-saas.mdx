## The catch in each tool, ours included

The tools that cost $0 at the start mean different things by it. The catch in each one, from each vendor's pricing page, checked October 12, 2026:

**[Affitor](https://affitor.com/pricing).** $0, with no subscription and no setup fee, until a program passes $10,000 in affiliate revenue (all-time, per program), then 3.5% of each affiliate sale. The 3.5% has no cap, so the fee grows with affiliate revenue. No importer: you invite partners by email or CSV, 50 at a time, and they get new links; past sales and unpaid commissions stay in your old tool. Payouts go by bank transfer or Wise after a manual review, not PayPal. Stripe is the native integration; Paddle, Lemon Squeezy and other billing systems send sales through the API or SDK. No bulk export of partners, clicks or commissions yet: each weekly invoice downloads as a PDF, and partners can download the transactions on their payout page as a CSV.

**[Endorsely](https://endorsely.com/pricing).** $0 on Starter, up to $1,000 a month in affiliate revenue, then $39 a month on Pro (up to $5,000 a month) and $99 a month up to $20,000. The cap is monthly: after $1,000 in monthly affiliate sales, you upgrade to keep tracking. Payouts are a PayPal bulk file you send yourself.

**[PromoteKit](https://www.promotekit.com/pricing).** $0 for your first 3 referrals, then $29 a month on Pro, up to $10,000 a month; above that, contact sales. 3 referrals is a trial in practice. Stripe is required. Optional payouts to affiliates' bank accounts add a [fee on top of the payout total](https://www.promotekit.com/docs/payouts/stripe-payouts#fees): 5% if you fund them from a bank account (ACH), 8% by credit or debit card.

**[Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing).** No monthly fee. Base fee of 5% + 50¢ per sale, as merchant of record, plus 3% on each affiliate-referred sale, from the first one. [Its fees page](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees) adds 0.5% on subscription payments and 1.5% each on international and PayPal payments. Tracks sales made through its own checkout. Affiliates pay 2% on their payouts.

**[Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing).** 14-day trial, then $49 a month on Starter (up to $7,500 a month) and $99 on Growth (up to $15,000). A trial, not a free plan: $49 a month whether or not affiliates sell. Optional Managed Payouts cost 3%.

**[FirstPromoter](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing).** 14-day trial, no card, then $49 a month on Starter (up to $5,000 a month) and $99 on Business (up to $15,000). [Its own site](https://firstpromoter.com/solutions/free-affiliate-marketing-software) says "There is no permanent free plan." Starter allows up to 1,000 affiliates.

**[Trackdesk](https://trackdesk.com/pricing).** 14-day trial, then $299 a month on Business and $499 on Enterprise. Some articles still describe a free plan. [Its help center](https://help.trackdesk.com/en/articles/11405479-limits-of-the-deprecated-free-plan) says the Free plan is no longer available to new users; existing users keep it.

The commissions you pay affiliates come on top with every tool.

## Five ways affiliate software is priced

Affiliate tools charge in five ways, and the price that matters is the one you pay at your stage. All prices checked Oct 12, 2026; each tool name links to its pricing page.

**Flat subscription with a cap.** [PromoteKit](https://www.promotekit.com/pricing) ($29/mo on Pro, up to $10,000/mo in affiliate revenue), [Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing) ($49/mo, $7,500/mo on Starter), [FirstPromoter](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing) ($49/mo, $5,000/mo on Starter), [Tolt](https://tolt.com/pricing) ($69/mo, $10,000/mo on Basic) and [Endorsely](https://endorsely.com/pricing) ($0 up to $1,000/mo, then $39/mo up to $5,000/mo). [Tapfiliate](https://tapfiliate.com/pricing/) caps conversions (500/mo on Launch) and [Post Affiliate Pro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/pricing/) tracking requests (10,000/mo on Starter). The bill is the same each month, and crossing a cap moves you to the next plan.

**Subscription plus a percentage.** [Awin](https://www.awin.com/us/pricing) ($49/mo plus 3.5% per transaction on Access), [Refersion](https://www.refersion.com/pricing/) ($39/mo plus 3% of affiliate sales on Launch), [Dub Partners](https://dub.co/pricing) ($90/mo plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH) and [Reditus](https://getreditus.com/pricing) ($149/mo plus 5% by card or 2% by invoice on the commissions you pay). Tolt adds 2% on automatic payouts.
