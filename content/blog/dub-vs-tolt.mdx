## Where Dub Partners is stronger

- **Payouts and tax forms on the entry plan.** Business sends partner payouts for you, and Dub says it handles W-9, W-8 and 1099 forms. On Tolt, automatic payouts and tax forms start on Growth at $99; Basic payouts are manual.
- **More seats.** Business includes 10 users, against 3 team members on Tolt Basic.
- **Importers.** Dub imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. Tolt's page for Rewardful users describes a customer that moved its program to Tolt in one click.
- **No minimum term stated.** Dub's pricing page says you can cancel at any time; Tolt's pricing page does not state a minimum term.
- **Links, analytics and payouts in one product**, since Dub Partners runs on Dub's link platform.
- **A marketplace listing and partner discovery**, on Enterprise. Tolt lists neither in its plan table.

The limits to know: Business covers $2,500 a month in partner payouts and holds 500 partners, and Dub charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH and 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise). When you hit your plan's payout limit you can't send more payouts until the next billing period; Advanced is $300 a month with up to $15,000.

## Where Tolt is stronger

- **Lower entry price.** Basic is $69 a month for up to $10,000 a month in affiliate revenue, against $90 a month for Dub's Business plan. Growth is $99 for up to $20,000 and Pro $199 for up to $50,000.
- **Lower payout fee.** Automatic payouts carry a 2% processing fee, against 5% by ACH or 8% by card on Dub.
- **Unlimited affiliates and referrals on every plan.** Dub Business holds 500 partners.
- **Paddle and Chargebee natively**, next to Stripe. Dub connects Stripe and Shopify and takes other billing through its API.
- **Payout methods on Growth.** PayPal, Wise, local bank, crypto and wire, plus W-9/W-8 collection and 1099 filing.
- **More than one program.** Basic includes 2 programs, Growth 5 and Pro unlimited.
- **A trial with no card**, 14 days, and a 30-day refund.

The limits to know: Basic is manual payouts, with 3 team members, and the pricing page does not state a minimum term.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose Tolt when you want unlimited affiliates, Paddle or Chargebee natively, and the lower payout fee: $69 a month for up to $10,000 a month in affiliate revenue, then $99 on Growth for automatic payouts and tax forms. Choose Dub Partners when you want payouts and tax forms from the entry plan, more teammates, or you already use Dub for links; budget $90 a month plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH, and $300 a month on Advanced once payouts pass $2,500 a month. Both offer a 14-day trial.

## FAQ

### How do Tolt and Dub prices compare?

On the entry plans, Tolt costs less. Tolt Basic is $69 a month for up to $10,000 a month in affiliate revenue, with manual payouts. Dub Partners Business is $90 a month for up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts, plus 5% on payouts funded by ACH or 8% by card. Tolt's automatic payouts, on Growth at $99 a month, carry a 2% fee. Prices checked October 12, 2026.

### Which one pays affiliates for you?

Dub Partners sends payouts on its Business plan. Tolt sends automatic payouts from its Growth plan, by PayPal, Wise, local bank, crypto and wire, with a 2% processing fee; on Basic you pay affiliates yourself (as of October 12, 2026).

### Do Dub and Tolt handle tax forms?

Dub says its Business plan handles W-9, W-8 and 1099 forms. Tolt collects W-9 and W-8 forms and files 1099s from its Growth plan at $99 a month (as of October 12, 2026).

### Can I move from Tolt to Dub?

Yes. Dub Partners imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. Moving the other way, Tolt's page for Rewardful users describes a customer that moved its program to Tolt in one click.
