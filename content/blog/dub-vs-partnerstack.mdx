## Where Dub Partners is stronger

- **A published price.** Business is $90 a month and Advanced $300, with a 14-day trial and 10% off yearly. PartnerStack confirms the plan and price during a demo, based on program size, features and support.
- **No minimum term.** Dub's pricing page says you can cancel your subscription at any time. PartnerStack's [SaaS agreement](https://www.partnerstack.com/legal/saas-agreement) renews automatically for one-year terms.
- **Stripe natively.** Dub has a native Stripe integration and credits sales to Stripe promotion codes. PartnerStack listens to Stripe through a webhook integration.
- **Importers.** Dub imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. PartnerStack's own importer creates or updates records from a CSV file.
- **Tax forms on Business.** Dub says it handles W-9, W-8 and 1099 forms. We could not check PartnerStack's tax-form handling.
- **Links, analytics and payouts in one product**, since Dub Partners runs on Dub's link platform.

The limits to know: Business holds 500 partners and 10 users and covers $2,500 a month in partner payouts, and Dub charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH and 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise). Its marketplace listing and partner discovery are on Enterprise.

## Where PartnerStack is stronger

- **A marketplace on the entry plan.** Launch includes PartnerStack Marketplace access, where active B2B partners can discover your program. Dub's marketplace listing and partner discovery are on Enterprise.
- **Unlimited partners and internal users.** Dub Business holds 500 partners and 10 users.
- **Built for B2B partners beyond affiliates.** On Launch you pick affiliate link tracking or lead and deal registration, with onboarding and training tools, CRM integrations and an MCP connector. Growth adds a learning management system and MDF management.
- **More billing systems.** PartnerStack integrates with Chargebee, Recurly and Paddle next to Stripe. Dub connects Stripe and Shopify and takes other billing through its API.
- **One monthly invoice for payouts.** PartnerStack consolidates approved commissions into a single monthly invoice and pays partners for you; they withdraw by direct deposit, PayPal or Stripe. The fee is not stated.

The limits to know: the price comes after a demo, the agreement renews for one-year terms, and its own FAQ says most teams are up and running in weeks, depending on program complexity and integrations.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose Dub Partners when you want to launch on a published price, keep the option to leave any month, and have Dub send payouts and handle tax forms; budget $90 a month plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH, and $300 a month on Advanced once payouts pass $2,500 a month. Choose PartnerStack when the partners you need are B2B affiliates, resellers and co-sell partners, you want them to find you in a marketplace from the entry plan, and a demo, a quote and a one-year term fit how you buy software.

## FAQ

### How much does PartnerStack cost compared with Dub?

PartnerStack does not publish prices: its Launch, Growth and Enterprise plans are priced after a demo, based on program size, features and support, on one-year renewing terms. Dub Partners publishes its prices: Business is $90 a month with up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts and Advanced is $300 with up to $15,000, plus 5% on payouts funded by ACH or 8% by card. Checked October 12, 2026.

### Which one helps you find partners?

PartnerStack includes access to the PartnerStack Marketplace on its Launch plan, where active B2B partners can discover your program. Dub Partners lists your program in its marketplace and helps you discover partners on Enterprise, its custom annual plan (as of October 12, 2026).

### Can I move from PartnerStack to Dub?

Yes. Dub Partners imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy. PartnerStack's own importer creates or updates records from a CSV file.

### Do Dub and PartnerStack work with Stripe?

Both do. Dub Partners has a native Stripe integration and can credit sales to Stripe promotion codes. PartnerStack connects to Stripe through a webhook integration and also integrates with Chargebee, Recurly and Paddle (as of October 12, 2026).

### Is there an option with no monthly fee?

Affitor, which we make, has no subscription: it costs nothing until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales, with no cap on affiliate revenue. It lists every program on its marketplace and reads Stripe sales with no code. It has no importer, so partners get new links, and it pays partners by bank transfer or Wise rather than PayPal (as of October 12, 2026).
