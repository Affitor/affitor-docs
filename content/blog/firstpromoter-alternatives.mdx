SaaS teams leave FirstPromoter for three reasons: the lowest entry-plan revenue cap of the flat-fee trackers here ($5,000/month in affiliate revenue before the $49 plan forces an upgrade), features that wait on a higher plan, and cookie-based attribution that loses referrals when the cookie dies before signup. If none of those hurt yet, keep FirstPromoter. Its entry plan already includes APIs and webhooks, recurring and multi-tier commissions, W-9/W-8BEN collection, fraud detection, and five native billing connections (Chargebee, Recurly, and Braintree included) that neither Affitor nor Rewardful matches. Plenty of programs never need to move.

This guide compares five alternatives worth shortlisting in 2026: Affitor, Rewardful, Tolt, Dub Partners, and PartnerStack. One disclosure before we start: we build Affitor. It is one of the five tools below, and this post tells you plainly where the others beat it.

## Why teams outgrow FirstPromoter

FirstPromoter's model is a flat subscription with revenue-capped plans. Per [firstpromoter.com/pricing](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing), checked October 2026: Starter is $49/mo for up to $5,000/mo in affiliate revenue, with 3 campaigns and up to 1,000 affiliates. Business is $99/mo for up to $15,000/mo, with unlimited campaigns and affiliates, 2 websites, and a custom dashboard domain. Enterprise starts at $149/mo above that. Team members are unlimited on every plan, there is a 14-day trial with no credit card required, and no transaction fee is listed on the pricing page.

The cap is the first thing that bites, and it bites earlier here than anywhere else in the flat-fee group. Rewardful's $49 plan covers $7,500/mo in affiliate revenue; Tolt's $69 plan covers $10,000/mo. FirstPromoter's $49 plan stops at $5,000/mo: a program doing modestly well crosses it within months, and the tool's price doubles not because you used more software but because your affiliates performed.

The second issue is what waits on a higher plan. The entry plan has the APIs, webhooks, MCP server (in beta), and the full commission engine. But a second website on its own domain needs Business (2 websites) or Enterprise (3), one-click PayPal payouts and a custom dashboard domain need Business, and managed payouts, where FirstPromoter pays affiliates for you, need [Enterprise 2 and up, for SaaS companies, subject to approval](https://help.firstpromoter.com/en/articles/9998853-how-managed-auto-payouts-work). If you run more than one product, Starter is not an option.

The third is attribution. FirstPromoter tracks with a cookie, with first- or last-click credit and a cookie life you set (up to 9,999 days). When the cookie is gone before signup (cleared, blocked, or the buyer switches devices), the referral is gone with it.

## What to evaluate in a replacement

Three questions separate the five tools below faster than any feature checklist.

**How does it charge?** Flat subscription, percentage of results, or both. Watch for double tolls: some tools charge a subscription and then add a fee on payouts or transactions on top.

**What happens when the cookie dies?** Every tool here starts from a partner click. The difference is what carries the referral afterwards: a browser cookie all the way to each sale, or a customer record that the signup ties to the partner, which later payments match against.

**Can your coding agent do the integration?** In 2026 a lot of Stripe SaaS integration work is done by AI agents. By October 2026 most tools here have an MCP server of some kind: Dub on Business, a Tolt connection to Claude, FirstPromoter in beta, PartnerStack on Launch, and Affitor in beta, while Rewardful's MCP server covers its developer docs. The question to ask is whether an agent can install tracking and then prove it works with a test sale.

## 1. Affitor: best for paying on results

![Affitor's Partners view: active partners and applications in one table with live stats](/blog/shots/partners-table-live.png)

Affitor is our product, so here is the model stated plainly: you pay nothing until your affiliate program pays you.

### Key features

Tracking starts like the rest: a partner click sets a first-party cookie, and the partner whose click creates the customer keeps that customer. What carries the referral after that is the signup: you send your own customer key, it rides Stripe Checkout and subscription metadata as `affitor_customer_key`, and later payments are matched to the customer rather than to the cookie. The attribution window is set per program, 60 days by default, and is checked once, at the first attributed payment: the click must be no older than the window at signup (or at that payment, if there was no signup). Renewals of an attributed customer are not re-checked. The chain is documented in [how Affitor tracking fits together](/docs/brand/tracking/tracking-overview).

The agent surface is live today: a `skill.md` runbook an agent can follow end to end, the `affitor` CLI, browser and server SDKs plus an MCP server (`@affitor/sdk` and `@affitor/mcp`, both labeled beta), and `npx affitor onboard`, which installs tracking and then fires a test click, lead, and sale through your live integration until it reports `integration_verified`. You (or your agent) get proof the integration works before a real affiliate joins. The full setup path is walked in [How to create a Stripe affiliate program](/blog/stripe-affiliate-program).

![Affitor advertiser dashboard: performance overview tracking revenue, partners, clicks, signups, and paid customers for one program](/docs/brand/dashboard.png)

Every live program is listed on the [Affitor marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace), and the Affitor team helps you recruit, onboard, and train partners ([affitor.com/join](https://affitor.com/join)). Day-to-day operations stay deliberately small: one partner table holds active partners and applications, and you approve each application by hand or set auto-accept.

### Pricing

$0/mo, no setup fee, and nothing per partner or per seat. You pay $0 on the first $10,000 of affiliate revenue per program, then 3.5% of the sales affiliates drove (checked Oct 2026, [affitor.com/pricing](https://affitor.com/pricing)). If your affiliates generate nothing, you pay nothing. There are no tiers and no revenue caps.

### Pros & cons

**Pros:** no subscription and no caps, so cost follows results; renewals stay attributed after the cookie is gone; a marketplace listing with recruiting help; an integration an agent can complete and test with a synthetic sale.

**Cons:** a percentage fee grows with your affiliate revenue, while a flat subscription stays flat until its cap, so price your own expected volume on both pricing pages before you choose. Affitor reads Stripe natively; on Chargebee or another billing system you wire it up through its API or SDK, while FirstPromoter connects five billing systems natively. Affitor collects each partner's tax form (W-8BEN, W-8BEN-E or W-9) before the first payout, as FirstPromoter does on every plan, and partners withdraw by bank transfer or Wise, where FirstPromoter also pays by PayPal, Stripe, and crypto.

**FirstPromoter:** $49 to $149+/mo from day one, cookie attribution with first- or last-click credit.
**Affitor:** $0/mo, 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales after the first $10,000 per program, a click cookie and then the signup customer key.

## 2. Rewardful: best for the simplest Stripe setup

Rewardful is a common flat-fee pick for Stripe SaaS, and against FirstPromoter specifically it wins on two concrete points: a higher entry-plan cap and unlimited affiliates on every plan.

### Key features

Setup is two scripts or a native one-click integration (Rewardful is a Premier Stripe Partner). The transaction fee is 0% on every plan, and every plan includes the REST API, first- or last-touch attribution, coupon code tracking, self-referral fraud detection, PayPal mass payouts, and Wise bulk payouts.

### Pricing

Per [rewardful.com/pricing](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing), checked October 2026: Starter is $49/mo for up to $7,500/mo in affiliate-attributed revenue, with 1 campaign and up to 2 team members. Growth is $99/mo for up to $15,000/mo, with unlimited campaigns and team members and a branded affiliate portal. Enterprise starts at $149/mo and covers up to $30,000/mo on its entry tier. Every plan has a 14-day trial, and annual billing gives 12 months for the price of 10.

### Pros & cons

**Pros:** $7,500/mo of headroom at $49 where FirstPromoter stops at $5,000; unlimited affiliates on every plan where FirstPromoter's Starter holds 1,000; a stated 0% transaction fee.

**Cons:** the plan table lists no multi-tier commissions and no W-9/W-8BEN collection (Rewardful collects tax info through its optional Managed Payouts, at 3%), and Starter allows 1 campaign and 2 team members where FirstPromoter's Starter has 3 campaigns and unlimited team members. Billing connections are Stripe and Paddle Classic: if you bill through Chargebee, Rewardful is not an option. Attribution is cookie-based (first- or last-touch, 60-day default), the same model FirstPromoter uses. For the full head-to-head, see [Rewardful vs FirstPromoter](/blog/rewardful-vs-firstpromoter); if you end up shortlisting against Rewardful instead, the [Rewardful alternatives guide](/blog/rewardful-alternatives) covers that direction.

**Rewardful:** $49 to $149+/mo, 0% transaction fee, API on every plan, Stripe and Paddle Classic.
**Affitor:** $0/mo, 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales after the first $10,000 per program, Stripe-native.

## 3. Tolt: best for unlimited affiliates on a flat fee

Tolt is a clean modern product in this peer group, and it removes two FirstPromoter limits at once: the affiliate count and the entry-plan cap.

### Key features

Unlimited affiliates and referrals on every plan, where FirstPromoter's Starter caps you at 1,000 affiliates. Payout rails are the broadest here: PayPal, Wise, local bank transfer, crypto, and wire, with auto payouts from the Growth plan up. At $99/mo Tolt gives you $20,000/mo of revenue headroom against FirstPromoter Business's $15,000. Tolt also has a [REST API](https://docs.tolt.com/introduction) and an MCP connection to Claude.

### Pricing

Per [tolt.com/pricing](https://tolt.com/pricing), checked October 2026: Basic is $69/mo for up to $10,000/mo in affiliate revenue with 2 programs, 3 team members, and manual payouts. Growth is $99/mo for up to $20,000/mo with 5 programs, auto payouts, and W-9/W-8 collection with 1099 filing. Pro is $199/mo for up to $50,000/mo with unlimited programs. 14-day trial, no credit card required, 30-day refund policy.

### Pros & cons

**Pros:** unlimited affiliates everywhere, wide payout rails, and double FirstPromoter's entry-plan revenue cap ($10,000 vs $5,000) for $20 more per month.

**Cons:** the nuance sits in the payout fees. Tolt markets "0% commission", and that claim has a footnote: auto payouts carry a 2% processing fee, and the Basic plan avoids the fee because its payouts are manual. Tax-form collection starts on Growth, where FirstPromoter collects forms on every plan, and Basic holds 3 team members where FirstPromoter's are unlimited. Tracking starts from a cookie with a set duration, plus a signup call that ties the click to your customer.

**Tolt:** $69 to $199/mo, plus 2% on auto payouts.
**Affitor:** $0/mo, one 3.5% fee on affiliate-driven sales after the first $10,000 per program.

## 4. Dub Partners: best for developer-first teams

Dub is affiliate infrastructure shaped like developer tooling.

### Key features

Dub's pricing page lists a REST API, an MCP server, native SDKs, and real-time event webhooks on every Partners plan, plus tax compliance handled for you, so you do not collect W-9, W-8 or 1099 forms yourself. If you already run Dub for link infrastructure, adding Partners keeps links, analytics, and payouts in one platform.

### Pricing

Per [dub.co/pricing](https://dub.co/pricing), checked October 2026: Partners needs a paid plan. Business is $90/mo with partner payouts up to $2,500/mo and up to 500 partners. Advanced is $300/mo with payouts up to $15,000/mo and unlimited partners. Enterprise is custom, billed annually, with unlimited payouts and a marketplace listing. The [payout fee](https://dub.co/help/article/partner-payouts) is 5% if you fund payouts by ACH, 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise).

### Pros & cons

**Pros:** API, MCP server, SDKs, and webhooks on the entry plan; tax compliance handled; one platform for links, analytics, and payouts.

**Cons:** the toll structure. You pay the subscription and a payout fee of 5% if you fund payouts by ACH, 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise), and the payout caps meter your program's growth by plan: a Business-plan program cannot pay partners more than $2,500 in a month, and lifting that ceiling to $15,000 means the $300/mo Advanced plan. The marketplace listing and partner network are Enterprise features.

**Dub Partners:** $90/mo plus a payout fee of 5% if you fund payouts by ACH or 8% by card, capped by plan.
**Affitor:** $0/mo plus 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales after the first $10,000 per program, no revenue cap.

## 5. PartnerStack: best for enterprise partner programs

PartnerStack is not really a FirstPromoter substitute; it is a different category, priced like one.

### Key features

A full partner-relationship-management suite ([pricing page](https://www.partnerstack.com/pricing), checked Oct 2026): PartnerStack Marketplace access on every plan, affiliate link tracking or lead and deal registration on Launch (both on Enterprise), partner payments, an MCP connector, and on Growth a learning management system, partner challenges, and MDF management. If you run affiliates, resellers, and referral partners as one program at scale, it is the serious option on this page.

### Pricing

PartnerStack does not publish prices. Per [partnerstack.com/pricing](https://www.partnerstack.com/pricing), checked October 2026, Launch, Growth, and Enterprise all start with a demo, and "Your final plan and pricing are confirmed during your demo." Pricing depends on program size, feature needs, and level of support. Its FAQ says there is no limit on partners or internal users.

### Pros & cons

**Pros:** marketplace distribution, multi-type partner motions, and enterprise operations no point tool on this page attempts.

**Cons:** the process. You see a price after a demo, and its FAQ says most teams are up and running in weeks. For Stripe, its docs describe a webhook that listens for charge and subscription events; we make no other claims about its attribution. For an early or mid-stage SaaS replacing a $49 tool, this is the wrong aisle; for a partnerships team that has outgrown a pure affiliate motion, it is the right one. If PartnerStack's process is the reason you are here, the dedicated [PartnerStack alternatives guide](/blog/partnerstack-alternatives) goes deeper.

**PartnerStack:** priced in a demo, full PRM suite.
**Affitor:** self-serve signup, $0/mo, affiliate programs without the PRM layer.

## Which one should you pick?

The honest segmentation is by stage, because the pricing models behave differently as affiliate revenue grows.

**$0–500K ARR: pick Affitor, or Rewardful if you want a known flat cost.** At this stage your affiliate program earns little or nothing yet, and a $49–$90 subscription is pure downside risk, doubly so on FirstPromoter, whose $5,000/mo cap is the first one you will hit. Affitor costs $0 until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, so the software decision needs no budget. If you would rather pay a predictable $49/mo, Rewardful gives you more headroom at the same price.

**$500K–5M ARR: compare at your own volume.** If affiliate-driven revenue is steady, a capped flat plan is predictable: FirstPromoter Business at $99/mo covers up to $15,000/mo with multi-tier commissions, tax-form collection, and fraud detection, and Tolt's $99 plan covers $20,000/mo with unlimited affiliates. If you bill through Chargebee or another non-Stripe provider, staying on FirstPromoter Business is often the honest answer. If affiliate revenue is still lumpy, Affitor's pay-on-results model keeps quiet months at $0.

**$5M+ ARR: think in programs, not trackers.** If you run affiliates plus resellers plus referral partners with a partner manager, PartnerStack's PRM suite is the real option. If it is still a pure affiliate motion, the Enterprise plans of FirstPromoter (from $149/mo) or Rewardful ($149+/mo), or Dub's custom Enterprise with its lower payout fees (3% by ACH, 6% by card), cover the volume.
