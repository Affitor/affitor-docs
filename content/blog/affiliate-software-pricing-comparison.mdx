## What the fee buys

Affitor's 3.5% is the whole price, and it pays for more than tracking. Every live program is listed on the [Affitor marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace), where affiliates browse programs to join. The Affitor team helps you recruit, onboard and train partners: [affitor.com/join](https://affitor.com/join).

Partners are paid by bank transfer or Wise after a review, and you fund payouts through a weekly invoice. Sales come in from Stripe through OAuth with no code, and from other billing systems through the API or SDK. There are no plan tiers, so every feature is on for every program.

Most flat-fee trackers stop at tracking: PromoteKit, Rewardful, FirstPromoter and Tolt list no marketplace or recruiting help in their plan tables. Endorsely lists programs in its affiliate network and finds up to 15 affiliates on its free Starter plan, Partnero has PartneroList and AI Discovery, and Dub Partners puts its marketplace on Enterprise. The [affiliates table](/blog/best-affiliate-software-saas#affiliates) in our full comparison checks this for all 19 tools.

## Five ways affiliate software is priced

Affiliate tools charge in five ways, and the price that matters is the one you pay at your stage. All prices checked Oct 12, 2026; each tool name in the table links to its pricing page.

**Flat subscription with a cap.** [PromoteKit](https://www.promotekit.com/pricing) ($29/mo on Pro, up to $10,000/mo in affiliate revenue), [Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/pricing) ($49/mo, $7,500/mo on Starter), [FirstPromoter](https://firstpromoter.com/pricing) ($49/mo, $5,000/mo on Starter), [Tolt](https://tolt.com/pricing) ($69/mo, $10,000/mo on Basic) and [Endorsely](https://endorsely.com/pricing) ($0 up to $1,000/mo, then $39/mo up to $5,000/mo). [Tapfiliate](https://tapfiliate.com/pricing/) caps conversions (500/mo on Launch) and [Post Affiliate Pro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/pricing/) tracking requests (10,000/mo on Starter). The bill is the same each month, and crossing a cap moves you to the next plan.

**Subscription plus a percentage.** [Awin](https://www.awin.com/us/pricing) ($49/mo plus 3.5% per transaction on Access), [Refersion](https://www.refersion.com/pricing/) ($39/mo plus 3% of affiliate sales on Launch), [Dub Partners](https://dub.co/pricing) ($90/mo plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH) and [Reditus](https://getreditus.com/pricing) ($149/mo plus 5% by card or 2% by invoice on the commissions you pay). Tolt adds 2% on automatic payouts.

**A minimum or a percentage, whichever is greater.** [Impact.com](https://impact.com/integrated-platform-prices/) Starter bills $30/mo or 2.5% of partner-driven sales, whichever is greater; Essentials is $500/mo including $2,500 in partner payouts, then 20% of payouts above that.

**A percentage after a threshold.** [Affitor](https://affitor.com/pricing) costs $0 until a program has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales, with no subscription and no cap.

**A quote.** [PartnerStack](https://www.partnerstack.com/pricing), [Partnerize](https://partnerize.com/pricing), [Everflow](https://www.everflow.io/pricing), [CJ Affiliate](https://www.cj.com/support) and [Rakuten Advertising](https://rakutenadvertising.com/affiliate/) price after a call or demo.

Which one costs less depends on how much your affiliates sell and for how long, which is why the tables above show scenarios with their assumptions instead of a single number.

On Affitor, nothing: there are no plan tiers, and each program gets its own first $10,000 at $0 before the 3.5% applies. Elsewhere, as of October 13, 2026: PromoteKit moves from its free plan (1 campaign) to Pro at $29/month, Rewardful from Starter (1 campaign) to Growth at $99/month, FirstPromoter needs Business at $99/month for a second website, Partnero moves from Starter (1 program) to Partner at $199/month, Tapfiliate from Launch (1 program) to Scale at $179/month, and Post Affiliate Pro from Starter (1 campaign) to Pro at $139/month. Tolt's Basic plan holds 2 programs, and Awin charges its $49 monthly platform fee per program, each with its own application.

### Does Affitor charge a monthly fee?

No. Affitor has no subscription, no setup fee and nothing per partner or per seat. A program pays nothing until it has made $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales. The $10,000 is counted per program and never resets.

### Which affiliate tools take a percentage of sales or payouts?

As of October 12, 2026: Awin charges 3.5% per transaction on its Access plan, Refersion 3% of affiliate sales on Launch, Dub Partners 5% of payouts funded by ACH (8% by card), Reditus 5% by card or 2% by invoice on the commissions you pay, and Tolt 2% on automatic payouts. Impact.com Starter bills $30/mo or 2.5% of partner-driven sales, whichever is greater. Rewardful (3%), Partnero (7%) and PromoteKit (5% if funded by bank, 8% by card) charge a fee when they run payouts for you. Affitor charges 3.5% of affiliate-driven sales after a program's first $10,000, with no subscription.

### Is a percentage fee better than a flat subscription?

It depends on how much your affiliates sell and for how long. A flat plan bills the same amount every month from the first day, and most plans cap the affiliate revenue they cover, so the price steps up as affiliates sell more. A percentage costs nothing while affiliates sell nothing and grows with the sales they bring. The scenario tables on this page show both, with every assumption stated.

### What does Affitor's 3.5% include?

Every feature, on every program: Stripe sales read through OAuth with no code, other billing systems through the API or SDK, a listing on the Affitor marketplace where affiliates browse programs, payouts to partners by bank transfer or Wise after a review, and help from the Affitor team to recruit, onboard and train partners.
