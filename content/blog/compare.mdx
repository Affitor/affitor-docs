## How we compare

Every price, fee and cap comes from the vendor's own pricing page or docs, with the date we checked it. Where a vendor does not publish something, the page says so instead of guessing. Caps are named for what they count: affiliate revenue for Rewardful, FirstPromoter and Tolt, partner payouts for Dub Partners.
