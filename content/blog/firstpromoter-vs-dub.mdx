## Where FirstPromoter is stronger

- **Lower entry price.** Starter is $49 a month, or $490 a year, against $90 a month for Dub's Business plan.
- **No fees listed.** FirstPromoter's pricing page lists no fees on top of the plan. Dub charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH and 8% by card (3% and 6% on Enterprise).
- **Five billing systems.** FirstPromoter works natively with Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee, Recurly and Braintree. Dub connects Stripe and Shopify and takes other billing through its API.
- **Unlimited team members on every plan**, against 10 users on Dub Business. Starter holds up to 1,000 affiliates; Dub Business holds 500 partners.
- **Commission rules.** Multi-tier commissions up to 3 levels, and API and webhooks on every plan.
- **Help moving in.** FirstPromoter's team helps migrate your promoters from another platform, and it offers a historical Stripe import when you migrate.

The limits to know: Starter covers up to $5,000 a month in affiliate revenue, with 3 campaigns. Business is $99 for up to $15,000, and it is where the custom dashboard domain and the in-app embedded dashboard start.

## Where Dub Partners is stronger

- **Payouts on the entry plan.** Business sends partner payouts for you, with the fee above. FirstPromoter's managed auto payouts need its Enterprise 2 plan or higher; direct PayPal payouts start on Business.
- **Tax forms.** Dub says it handles W-9, W-8 and 1099 forms on Business. FirstPromoter collects W-9 and W-8BEN forms in the affiliate portal on every plan, with no 1099 filing listed.
- **An MCP server on Business.** FirstPromoter's MCP server is in beta.
- **Importers.** Dub imports partners from Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Tapfiliate and Lemon Squeezy.
- **Links, analytics and payouts in one product**, since Dub Partners runs on Dub's link platform.
- **A marketplace listing and partner discovery**, on Enterprise. FirstPromoter lists neither in its plan table.

The limits to know: Business covers $2,500 a month in partner payouts, and when you hit your plan's payout limit you can't send more payouts until the next billing period. Advanced is $300 a month with up to $15,000.

## Which one should you pick?

Choose FirstPromoter when you bill through more than Stripe, want detailed commission rules, or need many teammates in the account: $49 a month for up to $5,000 a month in affiliate revenue, then $99 for up to $15,000. Choose Dub Partners when you want Dub to send payouts and handle tax forms from the entry plan, or you already use Dub for links; budget $90 a month plus 5% of payouts funded by ACH, and $300 a month on Advanced once payouts pass $2,500 a month. Both offer a 14-day trial, FirstPromoter's with no card, and both let you cancel any time.

## Where Affitor fits

## FAQ

### How do FirstPromoter and Dub prices compare?

On the entry plans, FirstPromoter costs less. FirstPromoter Starter is $49 a month with no fees listed on its pricing page and covers up to $5,000 a month in affiliate revenue. Dub Partners Business is $90 a month, covers up to $2,500 a month in partner payouts, and charges 5% on payouts you fund by ACH or 8% by card. Prices checked October 12, 2026.

### Which one works with Paddle or Chargebee?

FirstPromoter supports Stripe, Paddle, Chargebee, Recurly and Braintree natively. Dub Partners connects to Stripe and Shopify, and other billing systems send sales through its API (as of October 12, 2026).

### Which one pays affiliates for you?

Dub Partners sends partner payouts on its Business plan, with a 5% fee on payouts funded by ACH and 8% by card. FirstPromoter's managed auto payouts need its Enterprise 2 plan or higher, and direct PayPal payouts start on Business (as of October 12, 2026).
