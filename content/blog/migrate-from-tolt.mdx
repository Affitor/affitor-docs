To migrate from Tolt to Affitor, export your partners from Tolt, invite those partners to Affitor by name and email, and have each one switch to a new `?aff=` link. Keep Tolt running through its cookie window with every partner on only one tool, then move your Stripe connection and remove the Tolt script. Affitor does not import history yet.

I wrote this as the guide I would want if I were the one switching: what each step involves, where things can go wrong, and what Affitor does not do yet. We build Affitor, so read the last part as carefully as the rest.

## Before you start

You need admin access to Tolt, an Affitor account, and access to your site code and Stripe. Plan for the switch to take one full cookie window. Tolt lets you set the cookie duration per program, and B2B programs often use long ones, so look up yours before you set a date.

Make one decision up front: will you move every partner at once, or move them in batches? Both work. Batches are easier to watch, and they are the only way to keep a partner on one tool at all times without a scramble on cutover day.

## Step 1: Export your partners from Tolt

Pull your partner list out of Tolt and keep it as a spreadsheet. You will use it to invite partners and, later, to check that nobody was left behind. Tolt's API has a list-partners endpoint if you would rather script the export than copy it by hand.

For each partner, capture at least their name, email, commission rate, and unpaid balance. Affitor needs only the name and email. The other columns are for you: unpaid balances get paid out of Tolt, not Affitor.

## Step 2: Invite your partners to Affitor

Create your program on Affitor, set the commission to match what partners earn today, and connect tracking. The [quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) covers that part.

Then open **Partners**, click **Invite partners**, and paste the emails or import a CSV with `name,email` columns. You can invite up to 50 people per batch. Each partner gets an email with your program terms and an Accept button. They sign in, accept, and get their Affitor link right away. Invites expire after 30 days, so follow up with anyone who has not accepted. Details are in [Inviting partners](/docs/brand/quickstart/inviting-partners).

This is a real ask of your partners. Tell them before the invite arrives why you are moving and what changes for them.

## Step 3: Give every partner a new link

Tolt links carry Tolt's own tracking parameter, commonly `?via=`. Affitor links look like `yoursite.com/?aff=CODE`. Affitor does not read Tolt's parameter, so a partner's old link only counts in Tolt.

Ask each partner to replace the links they control: their bio, their newsletter footer, their pinned post. Old links buried in videos and old articles will keep crediting through Tolt until you remove its script in Step 6, and after that they stop tracking.

## Step 4: Run both tools through the cookie window

Leave the Tolt script on your site and add the Affitor tracking next to it. Partners who have moved send traffic with `?aff=`, partners who have not yet moved keep using their Tolt links, and each tool only tracks its own links.

Run like this for at least one full Tolt cookie window. Referrals that started in Tolt get the time to convert there, and new referrals from moved partners start in Affitor. If your Tolt window is long, moving partners in batches keeps the overlap manageable. The next section covers the rules that keep this safe.

## Step 5: Switch your Stripe connection

When the window has passed and every partner who is coming has moved, connect Stripe to Affitor as your only affiliate tool. Follow [Payment tracking](/docs/brand/tracking/payment-tracking-stripe) to pass the Affitor metadata on Checkout, and test one purchase end to end before you trust it.

Then settle the last Tolt payout run and disconnect Tolt from Stripe so it stops recording new commissions.

## Step 6: Remove the Tolt script

Remove the Tolt script from your site last. From this point, any Tolt link still out in the wild stops tracking, so send partners one more reminder to update old links before you do it. Cancel your Tolt plan once the final payouts are done.

## Run both in parallel without paying twice

Running two affiliate tools at once is normal during a move. It is safe when you follow one rule: **each partner is on one tool at a time.** Do not enroll the same partner in both Tolt and Affitor.

Here is what can go wrong if you do not:

- **The same partner is active in both tools.** They post both links, a customer clicks one and later the other, and each tool records the sale. You pay that partner twice for one customer.
- **Two partners claim the same customer.** A customer clicks partner A's Tolt link, then partner B's Affitor link, then buys. Each tool credits its own partner. Affitor does not check Tolt's records, and Tolt does not check Affitor's.
- **The numbers will not match.** Two tools with different windows and rules will credit some sales differently. That is expected, not a bug. Reconcile at the end of each month so every sale has a commission in at most one tool.

Affitor ignores Stripe sales it cannot connect to one of its own partners: no commission and no fee. That keeps Tolt's sales out of Affitor, but it does not stop the cases above, which is why the one-partner-one-tool rule matters.

## What Affitor does not do yet

I would rather you hear this from me than find it on cutover day.

- **No automatic import.** Affitor does not connect to Tolt and pull your partners, customers, or referrals. You invite partners by name and email, and each partner accepts.
- **Old links are not carried over.** Affitor only tracks `?aff=` links. Tolt links keep working only while the Tolt script stays installed.
- **No history import.** Past sales, clicks, and unpaid commissions stay in Tolt. Pay outstanding balances from Tolt before you close it.
- **No cross-tool duplicate check.** Affitor cannot see Tolt's data, so keeping each partner on one tool is your call, not something the software enforces.

## FAQ

### Can Affitor import my partners and sales from Tolt automatically?

No, not today. You bring partners over by inviting them by name and email (paste or CSV, up to 50 per batch), and each partner accepts the invite. Past referrals, sales, and unpaid commissions stay in Tolt; Affitor starts counting from the day you switch.

### Will my partners' existing Tolt links keep working?

They keep working in Tolt for as long as the Tolt script stays on your site. Affitor does not read Tolt's tracking parameter. Each partner gets a new Affitor link that uses `?aff=`, and you should ask partners to update the links they have posted.

### Can I run Tolt and Affitor at the same time?

Yes, and for most programs that is the safe way to switch. The rule is that each partner lives on one tool at a time. Do not enroll the same partner in both, or the same customer can earn a commission in each tool.

### How long should I run both tools?

At least as long as the cookie window set on your Tolt program. Long B2B windows mean a longer overlap, so check the setting before you pick a cutover date.

### How much does Affitor cost?

Affitor is free until your first $10k in affiliate revenue, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven revenue. There is no monthly subscription.
