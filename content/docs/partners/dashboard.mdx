The partner dashboard has four sections in the left menu: **Programs**, **Marketplace**, **Referrals** and **Payout**. **Messages** is the message icon in the top bar (a menu row on phones). This page shows what each one holds.

## Programs

**Programs** is your partner home. It lists the programs you joined or applied to:

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Active, or Pending while the brand reviews your application |
| **Clicks** | Visits through your referral links |
| **Signups** | Referred users who created an account |
| **Paid Signups** | Referred users who became paying customers |
| **Revenue** | Sales attributed to you |
| **Total Commission** | What you've earned |

**Search programs** and the **All** / **Active** / **Pending** filter narrow the list; the table footer shows how many programs you have. **Find programs** opens the Marketplace. On a pending application, **⋯** → **Withdraw application** removes it; you can apply again later from the Marketplace.

Until your partner setup is done, a **Finish setup** card above the list shows how many of its three steps are done and a **Continue:** button naming the next one.

## A program's page

Select a program to open its page. At the top, **Copy link** copies your referral link and **Message** opens a conversation with the brand. The page has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Clicks**, **Signups**, **Paid signups**, **Revenue** and **Commission** for **7d**, **30d**, **90d** or **All**, a chart, and your **Top links** |
| **Links** | Every link you made for this program with its clicks, signups, paid signups and revenue. **Create link** adds another |
| **Customers** | The people you referred: **Customer** (masked email and signup date), **Last purchase** and **Lifetime value** |
| **Commissions** | Each commission with its **Date**, **Type**, **Link**, **Sale**, **Commission** and **Status** (such as **Pending**, **Approved**, **Paid**, **Rejected** or **Refunded**) |

## Marketplace

The **Marketplace** lists the programs you can join. Each card has **Apply**, or shows **Joined** or **Applied**. See [Find and join programs](/docs/partners/quickstart/find-and-join-programs).

## Referrals

**Referrals** is the page for the Affitor Refer Program: your link for referring brands to Affitor, **Invite via email**, the brands you referred with their stage, and your **Earnings** (**On hold**, **Released**, **Lifetime**) with **Withdraw** and **Payout history**. See [Refer a brand to Affitor](/docs/partners/quickstart/get-your-referral-link#refer-a-brand-to-affitor) and [How referral rewards work](/docs/partners/referral-rewards).

## Messages

**Messages** holds your conversations with brands. **New message** starts one with any program. While your application to a program is pending, you can send it 3 messages until the brand answers.

## Payout

**Payout** shows your balance and **Payout history**, and **Withdraw** opens the withdrawal. It's covered next.

## Your account

Your profile row sits at the bottom of the left rail. Its **⋯** menu has **Your account**, with **Profile**, **Login and security** and **Payouts**. See [Account and payout settings](/docs/partners/account-settings).
