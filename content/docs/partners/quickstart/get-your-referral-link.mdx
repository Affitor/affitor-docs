Your referral link is the URL you share. Every click and sale through it is credited to you. You don't have to create it: Affitor generates it for you.

## Where to find it

Your referral link is created automatically the moment you're approved: no button to press, no setup. Open the program from **Programs** to find it.

If you don't see a link, the brand hadn't set a website for the program when you were approved, so no link was created. Once the brand saves a valid website, open the program again from **Programs**: the page creates your link on that visit.

**Copy link** at the top of the program's page copies it. Your links are also under **Top links** on **Overview** and on the **Links** tab, where **Create link** adds another.

## What your link looks like

Your link points at the brand's site with your tracking code attached. It takes one of these forms, depending on the brand's setup:

```text
https://brand.com/r/YOURCODE
https://brand.com?via=YOURCODE
https://brand.com?aff=YOURCODE
```

The `/r/` form is used when the brand has set up a short-link redirect on their site; otherwise the link uses the program's parameter, `?via=` or `?aff=`. All of them track the same way. The code is unique to you. When someone visits through it, Affitor records the click in a first-party cookie and ties any later signup or sale back to you. If the visitor was already referred by another partner, they stay credited to that first partner.

## Share it

Drop your link anywhere you reach an audience: content, social posts, communities, newsletters, or direct outreach. There's nothing else to install.

:::tip

Share the same link everywhere. Affitor tracks every click and conversion on it, so you can watch performance build in your dashboard.

:::

## Refer a brand to Affitor

Refer a brand has its own link, separate from your program links: `affitor.com/r/<username>` once you set a username, otherwise `affitor.com/r/<your-code>`. There is nothing to apply for. Get it on your **Referrals** page with **Get my link**, or with **Refer a brand → Get my link** on the Marketplace. On the brand side, **Settings** → **Refer a brand** shows the same link and the brands you referred, and **Refer a brand** in the account menu opens **Referrals**. **Set a username** (or **Edit username**) under the link opens [your username](/docs/partners/account-settings#username).

When you are signed in, the **Affitor Refer Program** card (**Get link**), its slide in the **Featured** strip, and **Get your referral link** on its program page open **Referrals** with your link already loaded.

A brand that opens it lands on Affitor's home page with "**your name** invited you" above an email field to start sign-up. A brand that signs up through it is credited to you under the [Affitor Refer Program terms](https://affitor.com/legal/refer-program).

### Invite a brand by email

**Invite via email**, next to **Copy** on **Referrals**, sends your link for you. In **Invite a brand**, type up to 5 addresses in **Send to**, check the **Preview**, and select **Send invite**.

- Affitor sends the email from `noreply@affitor.com`, with the subject "**your name** invited you to Affitor"; replies go to your email. Its **Start your program** button opens your Refer a brand link.
- Each address can be invited once every 30 days, whoever sends the invite. An address that already runs a brand on Affitor, or that is not valid (including your own), is not sent.
- You can invite up to 20 addresses in 24 hours, skipped ones included. Past that, the dialog says "You've reached today's invite limit. Try again tomorrow."
- After sending, a message lists who got the invite and why any address was skipped.

## What happens next

As people click and convert, your **Clicks**, **Signups**, **Paid signups**, **Revenue** and **Commission** fill in on the program's **Overview**. Track them to learn what's working.
