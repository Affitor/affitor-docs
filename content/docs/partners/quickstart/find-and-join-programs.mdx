Your first move after signing up: open the **Marketplace** and join a program. This is the step that turns a new account into an active partner.

## 1. Browse the Marketplace

Open the **Marketplace** from your dashboard, or go to **[affitor.com/marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace)**. It's public — you can browse programs before you even log in.

Each program card shows the commission in one line (for example *20% · 12 months*), how many partners have joined with the initials of its top partners, or **New program** when nobody has joined yet. On the right you see **Apply**, or **Joined** / **Applied** once you are in or waiting; a program your own brand runs says **Your program**.

The **Affitor Refer Program** card says **Get link** where other cards say **Apply**: you refer brands to Affitor without applying (see [Refer a brand to Affitor](/docs/partners/quickstart/get-your-referral-link#refer-a-brand-to-affitor)). The **Featured** strip above the search box always starts with it.

### Search, sort and filter

Search programs from the box above the cards. **Sort** orders the list by **Newest**, **Highest commission**, **Most partners** or **Longest cookie**. **Filter** narrows it to **Verified only** programs (see below), and by **Commission type** (recurring, one-time, per action), **Category** and **Cookie window** (30+, 60+ or 90+ days); you can tick several types and categories, and one cookie window. On a computer the menu shows how many programs each choice has. Your choices show as chips under the bar; remove one, or **Clear** them all. They are kept in the page address, so a reload or a shared link shows the same list.

### Verified programs

A blue check mark after a program's name, or a **Verified** label on its program page, means Affitor has checked how the program runs on Affitor. It is not a review of the product. Hover or tap the mark to see what was checked:

- Affitor has recorded real clicks or sales
- The brand's payments are connected to Affitor
- The brand is up to date on Affitor invoices

Once partners of a program have been paid through Affitor, the list adds **Partners have already been paid through Affitor**.

Affitor rechecks automatically, so the mark goes away by itself when a check stops holding. To see only these programs, choose **Verified only** in **Filter**.

## 2. Open a program

Click any program to see its full details — commission terms, how long commissions last, and what the product does.

## 3. Apply

Click **Apply to promote** (**Apply** on a Marketplace card). The only field is an optional **How will you promote it?**

:::tip

You can apply with **one click** — the message is optional. Don't let a blank field stop you.

:::

## 4. Get approved

What happens next depends on the program:

- **Instant approval** — the program auto-accepts partners. You'll see a **"You're in"** confirmation with your referral link right away.
- **Manual review** — the brand reviews applications. You'll see **"Application sent"** and an **Applied** badge on the program card while you wait. Once the brand approves you, you're enrolled automatically.

:::note

Affitor doesn't email you at every step yet. Check **Programs**: a program waiting for review shows as **Pending**.

:::

## What happens next

Once you're approved, you're enrolled in the program. Open it from **Programs** to **grab your referral link**: it's generated for you automatically.
