Partners promote SaaS products and earn commission on the customers they refer. This guide takes you from a brand-new account to your first shareable referral link.

> **Key terms:**
> - **Partner**: you: the affiliate, creator, agency, or promoter sending traffic
> - **Brand**: the SaaS company whose program you join
> - **Referral link**: your URL with a referral code; recording visits and later attribution depend on the brand's tracking setup

## How it works

<Flow>
  <FlowStep caption="Browse programs and apply in one click.">
    Find a program in the **Marketplace** → **apply**
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep caption="Auto-accept programs approve instantly; others are reviewed by the brand.">
    Get **approved** → you're enrolled
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep caption="Your link is generated automatically — no setup needed.">
    Open the program → **copy your referral link**
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep caption="Visits can be recorded when tracking runs on the destination page.">
    Share your link → **earn commission**
  </FlowStep>
</Flow>
