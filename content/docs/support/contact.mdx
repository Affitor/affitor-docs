Pick the address that fits your message, so it reaches the right people.

## Email

| For | Address |
|---|---|
| Help with your program, tracking, payouts or your account | [support@affitor.com](mailto:support@affitor.com) |
| Partnerships | [partnerships@affitor.com](mailto:partnerships@affitor.com) |
| General mail and press | [hello@affitor.com](mailto:hello@affitor.com) |

For help, include what the [support page](/docs/support) lists so we can look into it without asking again. For press, use the subject line **Press Inquiry**.

## For brands

To set up a program, start with [Getting started](/docs/getting-started). For a question about a program you run, email [support@affitor.com](mailto:support@affitor.com).

## For partners and creators

To join a program, find it in the [Affitor marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace) and select **Apply to promote** on the program's page. For a question about a program you promote or your payouts, email [support@affitor.com](mailto:support@affitor.com).

Find us on [X @affitor_ai](https://x.com/affitor_ai) and [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/affitor) for product updates.
