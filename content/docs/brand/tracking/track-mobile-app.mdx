A mobile app is tracked with the same model as a [desktop app](/docs/brand/tracking/track-desktop-app): record the partner click on your website, carry the click ID into the app once, send a lead at signup, and send each purchase from your backend. What changes is where people pay.

## Where your customers pay

| Customers pay | Who learns about the payment | How it reaches Affitor |
|---|---|---|
| On your website (web checkout) | Your payment provider | As for any web product: [Stripe](/docs/brand/tracking/payment-tracking-stripe), or your server calling [Track Sale](/docs/api-reference/track-sale) |
| Inside the app, through the App Store or Google Play | Apple or Google, then your backend or your subscription service | Your backend calls [Track Sale](/docs/api-reference/track-sale) and [Track Refund](/docs/api-reference/track-refund) |

Which payment method your app may use is set by each store's rules, for example Apple's [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) section 3.1.

## What Affitor does not do

- There is no iOS or Android SDK. `@affitor/sdk` is for JavaScript in the browser, React and a Node server.
- Affitor does not create deep links or deferred deep links, and does not read install referrers for you.
- Affitor has no connection to the App Store, Google Play or a subscription service. Purchases made there reach Affitor only when your backend sends them to the Tracking API.

## Step 1: Send partners to your website

Partner links open a page on your website, where the [click is recorded](/docs/brand/tracking/click-tracking) and the click ID is kept. A link straight to a store page runs none of your code, so no click is recorded. From your page, send the visitor on to the store.

## Step 2: Carry the click ID into the app

The app needs the click ID once, before or at account creation. These are the platform tools you can build on; each is set up in your app, not in Affitor:

| Way | Platform documentation |
|---|---|
| Sign up on the web, then sign in to the app | None needed: the account is created where the click ID already is |
| Open the installed app from a link on your site, with the click ID in the URL | Apple [universal links](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-universal-links-in-your-app), Android [App Links](https://developer.android.com/training/app-links) |
| Read the referrer of an install from Google Play | Google [Play Install Referrer API](https://developer.android.com/google/play/installreferrer) |
| A one-time code your site shows and your app asks for | Your own: your server maps the code to the click ID |

Save the click ID on the user record. At account creation your server sends [`POST /api/v1/track/lead`](/docs/api-reference/track-lead) with the `click_id` and your user ID as `customer_key`, as in [Step 4 of the desktop guide](/docs/brand/tracking/track-desktop-app#step-4-send-the-lead-at-signup).

## Step 3: Send store purchases from your backend

Apple and Google notify your backend, directly or through a subscription service. Your backend then sends each purchase or renewal as one sale, keyed by the `customer_key` you sent with the lead, and each refund as one refund.

| Source | What it tells your backend |
|---|---|
| Apple [App Store Server Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreservernotifications) | In-app purchase events, including purchases, subscription renewals and refunds |
| Google Play [Real-time developer notifications](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/rtdn-reference) | That a purchase or subscription changed state; Google asks you to call the Play Developer API for the details |
| A subscription service, for example [RevenueCat webhooks](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/integrations/webhooks/event-types-and-fields) | Events such as `INITIAL_PURCHASE`, `RENEWAL` and `CANCELLATION` (which also covers refunds), with the subscriber's `app_user_id` |

For each event, look up your user and send:

- a new purchase or a renewal: [`POST /api/v1/track/sale`](/docs/api-reference/track-sale) with a unique `transaction_id` (for example the store's transaction ID), your user ID as `customer_key`, `amount_cents` and `currency`;
- a refund: [`POST /api/v1/track/refund`](/docs/api-reference/track-refund) with the `transaction_id` of the sale it refunds.

The request bodies are the same as in [Step 5 of the desktop guide](/docs/brand/tracking/track-desktop-app#step-5-send-payments-renewals-and-refunds-from-your-server). Commissions then follow your [hold period](/docs/brand/quickstart/commission-approval-cash-flow).
