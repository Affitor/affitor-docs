Every program on the Affitor marketplace is at one of three levels: **Live**, **Launch** or **Verified**. The level decides where partners find your program and what they see next to its name.

## The three levels

| Level | What your program needs | What partners see |
|---|---|---|
| Live | You pressed **Go live** | Your program page, where partners apply and get their links. From November 27, 2026, the program shows in the marketplace when partners turn on **All programs** |
| Launch | Live, plus payments, a complete program page and terms (below) | Your program in the default list, the one partners see when they open the marketplace |
| Verified | Launch, plus the Verified checks (below) | A blue check mark after your program name |

The program count on the marketplace counts every live program, at every level.

## The default list changes on November 27, 2026

From **Friday, November 27, 2026, 5:00 PM PST**, the default list only includes programs at the Launch level. To be in it on that day, complete your program by **Thursday, November 26, 2026, 5:00 PM PST**.

From that time, a program that is not complete stays live: partners find it when they turn on **All programs** in the marketplace filter, and its card says **Pre-launch**. Its page keeps the same address. A program you or our team hid from the marketplace stays hidden.

Affitor checks programs again on its own. When yours meets every condition, it joins the default list. There is nothing to request.

## What the Launch level needs

### Payments
One of these:
- The program owner [connects Stripe](/docs/brand/tracking/payment-tracking-stripe) on this program in **Program settings > Payments**, with an account that accepts payments.
- Your server [sends sales](/docs/api-reference/track-sale) to Affitor, and at least one real paid sale has come in for a customer referred by a partner outside your team.

Test sales don't count.

### Program page
In **Program settings > Business**:
- **Business name**.
- **Website**.
- **Logo**: an uploaded logo. A logo shown from your website does not count.
- **About**: at least 40 characters about your product.

### Terms
In **Program settings > Commission**:
- A commission type and rate.
- An **Attribution window** of 1 to 365 days.
- A **Hold period** of 0 to 31 days.

## The Verified badge

A Verified program shows a blue check mark after its name. Partners can pick **Verified only** in the marketplace filter to see these programs.

The badge is automatic and follows your data. It shows while all of these hold, and goes away by itself when one stops:
- Your program is live and its website is an `https://` address.
- Your payments are connected: Stripe on the program, accepting payments, or a real sale your server sent in the last 90 days.
- Affitor has recorded at least one real click, lead or sale.
- No Affitor invoice is overdue.

Affitor's team can also remove the badge from a program.

When partners have already been paid through Affitor, the badge also says so.

From November 27, 2026, only programs at the Launch level show the badge.
