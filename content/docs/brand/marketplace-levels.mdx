Every program on the Affitor marketplace is at one of three levels: **Live**, **Launch** or **Verified**. The level decides where partners find your program and what they see next to its name.

## The three levels

| Level | What your program needs | What partners see |
|---|---|---|
| Live | You pressed **Go live** | Your program page. Partners can apply and get their links |
| Launch | Live, plus payments, a complete program page and terms (below) | Your program in the default list, the one partners see when they open the marketplace |
| Verified | Launch, plus the Verified checks (below) | A blue check mark after your program name |

A live program that is not yet at Launch stays live. Partners find it when they turn on **All programs** in the marketplace filter, and its card says **Pre-launch**. Its page keeps the same address. A program you or our team hid from the marketplace stays hidden. The program count on the marketplace counts every live program.

Affitor checks programs again on its own. When yours meets every condition, it joins the default list. There is nothing to request.

## What the Launch level needs

### Payments
One of these:
- Stripe is connected on the program and your Stripe account can accept payments. If Stripe still asks you to finish setting up your account, finish it there first.
- Your server [sends sales](/docs/api-reference/track-sale) to Affitor, and at least one real paid sale has come in.

Test sales don't count, including a test sale you send from **Settings > Tracking**. A purchase by your team, or by a partner for themselves, doesn't count either. A sale Affitor held for your review counts once you confirm it came from a real customer.

### Program page
- **Business name**.
- **Website**: an `http://` or `https://` address.
- **Logo**: a logo you uploaded, or a logo we find on the website set on your program page. A logo we find counts as soon as we find it; there is nothing to save. If you upload a logo, it is used instead. With no logo, your program can't reach Launch.
- **About**: at least 40 characters about your product.

### Terms
- A commission on sales: a type and a rate.
- An attribution window of 1 to 365 days.
- A hold period of 0 to 31 days.

New programs start with an attribution window of 60 days and a hold period of 15 days, which meet these terms.

## How to check your program

There is no single screen for your level yet. Check each condition where it lives:

| Condition | Where | Who can change it |
|---|---|---|
| Business name, website, logo, About | **Settings > Business** | Members, Admins and the Owner |
| Commission, attribution window and hold period | **Settings > Commission**, the **Terms** card (the windows under **Change**) | Admins and the Owner |
| Stripe | **Settings > Tracking**, **Count sales**, **Connect Stripe** | The Owner |
| Sales from your server | **Settings > Tracking**, **Count sales**, **Server API** | Your developer, with an [API key](/docs/brand/settings) |
| Live | **Settings > Status**: the card shows **Live** | Members, Admins and the Owner |

Then open the [marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace) with **All programs** on. If your program's card says **Pre-launch**, one of the conditions above is still missing.

## The Verified badge

A Verified program shows a blue check mark after its name. Partners can pick **Verified only** in the marketplace filter to see these programs.

The badge is automatic and follows your data. It shows while all of these hold, and goes away by itself when one stops:
- Your program is live and its website is a public `https://` address.
- Your brand account is active.
- Your payments are connected: Stripe on the program, accepting payments, or a real sale your server sent in the last 90 days.
- Affitor has recorded at least one real click, lead or sale.
- No Affitor invoice is overdue.
- Affitor has not removed the badge from your program.

Only programs at the Launch level show the badge. When partners have already been paid through Affitor, the badge also says so.
