Help your partners introduce SaaS brands to Affitor. Share the public referral page, then let each partner get their own personal link. Your program stays the same.

## Step 1: Open the sharing dialog

Open your program's **Overview**, select **Share**, then **Share Affitor with your partners**. **Share your program** still shares your own program page.

If your workspace does not show the Affitor sharing action yet, you can share the [public referral page](https://affitor.com/marketplace/referral-program) directly.

## Step 2: Copy the page or message

Choose **Copy link** to copy this public opportunity page:

```text
https://affitor.com/marketplace/referral-program
```

Choose **Copy message** to copy the message shown in the dialog, including the same URL. Paste it into the channel where you talk with your partners. Nothing is sent automatically.

Wait for **Link copied** or **Message copied** before pasting. If your browser blocks clipboard access, the dialog shows an error; select the link or message and copy it manually, or retry.

## Step 3: Let each partner get their own link

The shared page explains the existing Affitor referral program. A visitor can choose **Sign up to get your link** or use **Sign in** with an existing account. A signed-in visitor can choose **Get your referral link**.

The account links request a return to **Referrals** to prepare that person's link. If an external sign-in provider lands you elsewhere, open [Referrals](https://affitor.com/partner/referrals?link=1) and choose **Get my link**. This program does not require an application approval. If the link could not be loaded, use **Get my link** to retry.

After applying to an ordinary brand program, you may also see **Refer brands to Affitor** with **Get my Affitor referral link**. This is a separate opportunity: your original application's approval or pending state stays the same, and its original next action is still available.

## Step 4: Use the personal link to introduce a brand

On **Referrals**, each person copies **Your link** with **Copy** and sends that personal link to a SaaS founder or team they want to introduce. The personal invitation uses `affitor.com/r/<username-or-code>`.

The public opportunity page recruits people who want their own link. Their personal link is what they use to introduce a brand. Check the [program terms](https://affitor.com/legal/refer-program) for the current reward rules.
