Setup takes your program from created to live in five steps: **Business**, **Commission**, **Tracking**, **Payments** and **Go live**. It opens at the Business step as soon as you [create your program](/docs/brand/quickstart/create-account), at `affitor.com/brand/setup/business`.

## The flow at a glance

<Flow>
  <FlowStep>**Business**: business name, website, program URL and an optional logo.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep>**Commission**: what partners earn, the attribution window, the hold period and how new partners join.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep>**Tracking**: install the click tracker on your site. Optional for Go live.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep>**Payments**: connect Stripe, or send one sale from your server.</FlowStep>
  <FlowStep>**Go live**: publish the program once Business, Commission and Payments are done.</FlowStep>
</Flow>

The top of each step shows **Step N of 5** and **Save and exit**. **Continue** saves the step and opens the next one. **Save and exit** saves Business or Commission first, then opens that page in Settings; the other steps have nothing to save and open their Settings page directly. On a wide screen the step list sits on the left, where a done step shows a check (Business also shows your website, Commission your rate), and **What partners see** previews your program card on the right.

The same five pages are also **Settings** → **Program settings**, so anything you set here stays editable there. Until the program is live, Program settings shows how many of the five steps are done. After launch, each page saves in place with no **Back** or **Continue**, and **Go live** is named **Status**.

## Step 1: Describe your business

The **Business** step (**Your business**) sets what partners see first.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Business name** | Yes | 1 to 80 characters |
| **Website** | Yes | Your product or site URL. `example.com` is saved as `https://example.com` |
| **Program URL** | Yes | Your public program page, `affitor.com/marketplace/<handle>`. Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, up to 48 characters. You can change it later |
| **Logo** | No | PNG, JPG or SVG, square, under 5 MB |
| **Tagline** | No | In Settings only (**Program settings** → **Business**): one line on your marketplace card, up to 160 characters |

**Continue** saves only the fields you changed and moves on once every save succeeds; an error shows under its field. The step is done once the program has a business name and a website.

## Step 2: Set your commission

The **Commission** step sets what partners earn and how they join.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Type** | Yes | **One-time** pays on the first payment, **Recurring** monthly for the months in **For**, **Lifetime** on every payment |
| **Rate** | Yes | Percent of the payment, 0 to 100 with up to 2 decimals |
| **For** | Yes, for **Recurring** | How many months the partner keeps earning: a whole number from 1 to 36. Shown only for **Recurring** |
| **Attribution window** | Yes | How long after a click a signup still counts: 1 to 365 days |
| **Hold period** | Yes | Days until a commission is payable, to cover refunds: 0 to 31 |
| **New partners** | Yes | **Approve all** approves every application; **Review each** lets you review each one |

Once the rate is valid, a line shows what a $100 payment gives the partner, for example "$20 to the partner, monthly for 12 months". If your saved terms are ones this form cannot show exactly (for example a fixed amount), the step shows them as **Current terms** and keeps them unless you select **Set new terms**.

The step is done once a sale commission is saved and a **New partners** choice is set. To pay some partners a different rate, use [groups](/docs/brand/groups).

## Step 3: Install tracking

The **Tracking** step (**Install tracking**) puts the click tracker on your site. Pick an **Install method**:

- **Your agent**: select **Connect your AI** to connect Claude, Cursor or another AI app and let it add the tracker. Shown only when AI agents are available for your program.
- **Script tag**: paste the tag in `<head>` on every page of your site.
- **npm**: install the SDK and wrap your root layout.

The snippets on the page already carry your Program ID:

```html
<!-- Include Affitor Tracking Script -->
<script
  src="https://api.affitor.com/js/affitor-tracker.js"
  data-affitor-program-id="YOUR_PROGRAM_ID"
  data-affitor-debug="false">
</script>
```

```bash
npm install @affitor/sdk
```

```tsx
// app/layout.tsx (or your root layout)
import { AffitorProvider } from '@affitor/sdk/react';

export default function RootLayout({ children }) {
  return (
    <html>
      <body>
        <AffitorProvider programId="YOUR_PROGRAM_ID">
          {children}
        </AffitorProvider>
      </body>
    </html>
  );
}
```

Below the snippets, **Tracker on** your site shows **Waiting for the first visit**, **Tracker not found on your site yet**, or **First click** and how long ago it arrived. **Check now** asks Affitor to look for the tracker on your website again. Nothing is saved on this step: **Continue** turns on after the first click, and **Skip for now** moves on to Payments.

The step is done once Affitor finds the tracker on your website or receives real tracking events. Signup tracking is a separate install: see [signup tracking](/docs/brand/tracking/lead-tracking-signup), and [click tracking](/docs/brand/tracking/click-tracking) for the full click guide.

## Step 4: Connect payments

The **Payments** step (**Connect payments**) sets how Affitor records your sales. Choosing an option saves it right away. Only the program owner can choose the method or connect Stripe; other members see its state and "Only the program owner can connect payments."

| Option | On the page | What to do |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Stripe** | One click. No checkout code. | Select **Connect Stripe**. Stripe opens in a new tab; finish there and this page updates when you come back. If you started and did not finish, the button reads **Finish in Stripe**. Once connected, the page shows **Connected** |
| **Server API** | Your backend sends each sale. | Run the test sale below once from your server. The page shows **Waiting for a test sale from your server** until it arrives, then **Received**. **Check again** reads the status now |

The Server API test sale uses your program API key ([where to find it](/docs/brand/settings#step-2-find-your-api-key)). With `"livemode": false` it is a test sale and creates no commission:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.affitor.com/api/v1/track/sale \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_PROGRAM_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "click_id": "clk_abc123",
    "customer_key": "user_123",
    "amount_cents": 4900,
    "currency": "usd",
    "transaction_id": "txn_test_001",
    "livemode": false
  }'
```

Once payments are connected, **Check Affitor's tracking** offers **Run check**: Affitor sends a test click, signup and sale through your program and shows each as **Matched**, **Not matched**, **Wrong partner** or **Waiting**. It is a pipeline check and does not count toward Go live.

**Refunds and chargebacks**: with Stripe, Affitor reads refunds and disputes from your connected account. With the Server API, report each refund with [Track a refund](/docs/api-reference/track-refund).

The step is done when Stripe is connected, or, with the Server API, when Affitor has received a sale from your server. **Skip for now** moves on to Go live, which shows what is still missing.

## Step 5: Go live

**Go live** publishes your program on the marketplace. The page lists four rows:

| Row | Needed to go live | Done when |
|-----|-------------------|-----------|
| **Business** | Yes | the program has a business name and a website (the logo is optional) |
| **Commission** | Yes | a sale commission is saved |
| **Click tracking** | No | Affitor finds the tracker on your website or receives real events; until then the row reads "Optional · no visit seen yet" |
| **Payments** | Yes | Affitor can record your sales (see below) |

A row that is not done has **Fix** (**Set up** for click tracking), which opens its step. The page also shows the address your program page opens at. **Go live** turns on once the three required rows are done, and Affitor checks them again when you select it.

Payments counts as connected in one of two ways, matching the method you chose on the **Payments** step:

- **Stripe**: your Stripe account is connected to the program.
- **Server API**: your server has sent at least one sale for this program to `POST /api/v1/track/sale`. A test sale counts (send it with `"livemode": false`), so you can go live before your first real customer. **Run check** does not count; the sale has to come from your server.

Click and signup tracking are not required to go live. Install them before you invite partners so their traffic is attributed.

If the program is paused for an overdue invoice, Go live stays blocked and the page links to **Open billing**; pay the invoice first.

Once live, the page says your program is live and shows its link with **Copy link**, plus **Open dashboard** and **Invite partners**.
