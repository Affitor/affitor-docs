## Invoice billing

**Step by step:**

1. Customer purchases through your existing checkout.
2. You receive payment in your own payment stack.
3. Affitor attributes and validates the conversion.
4. Commission is created according to your program rules.
5. Advertiser billing is handled through Affitor's invoice workflow.
6. Commission moves through the hold period and, once approved, into the partner's balance. The partner can withdraw it once you pay the invoice that covers it.

Customer money never passes through Affitor. The money that does move through Affitor is the weekly invoice (approved commissions plus the platform fee), which you pay to Affitor LLC, and the partner withdrawals Affitor sends out:

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart LR
  A[Customer pays you] --> B[Affitor attributes the sale]
  B --> C[Commission pending]
  C -->|hold period ends| D[Commission approved]
  D --> E[Partner balance credited]
  E --> F[Partner requests a withdrawal]
  F --> G[Affitor approves and sends it]
  D --> H[Weekly invoice to you]
  H --> I[You pay Affitor LLC]
  I -->|unlocks the withdrawal| F
`} />

The partner's balance is credited when the commission is approved, but the partner can withdraw it only after you pay the invoice that covers it.

## Validation Before Commission

Affitor validates each attributed sale before the commission workflow proceeds.

Checks include:
- Valid attribution
- No duplicate sale event
- In-window attribution
- Matching program/customer relationship

## Approval and Hold Workflow

![The Commissions page tracks every commission through its lifecycle](/docs/brand/commissions.png)

Every commission moves through the same lifecycle:

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart LR
  A[Sale tracked] --> B[Pending]
  B -->|hold period ends| C[Approved]
  B -->|you reject it| X[Rejected]
  B -->|partial refund| R[Refunded]
  B -->|full refund| V[Reversed]
  C -->|weekly invoice| D[Invoiced]
  D -->|invoice marked paid| E[Collected]
  class X,R,V do
  class E ok
`} />

**Approved** is when the commission is added to the partner's balance. **Invoiced** and **Collected** track your side of the bill: the partner can withdraw the commission once it is **Collected**, which happens when you pay the invoice.

The **Commissions** page shows a count for **All** and for each status that has commissions; select a count to filter the list, or use **All statuses** in the filter. Each row names the customer by a masked email, or by **Customer #** and its number when there is no email, or says **Customer not recorded**. **Customers** is no longer a separate page: old Customers links open **Commissions**.

The program's hold period, set under **Commission** in [program settings](/docs/brand/settings), controls when commissions are approved. A commission approves automatically when the hold period ends (later, if you extend the hold), unless you approve or reject it first from the **Commissions** page. A test commission, a $0 commission on a payment after the commission term has ended, and one earned by someone on your own team (the brand owner or a workspace member) are not approved on their own.

### Common uses
- Reduce refund risk: a refund that lands during the hold is removed before it ever reaches the partner's balance
- Give yourself time to reject suspicious commissions before they approve
- Extend the hold on a commission you are still checking

## Refund Handling

What happens depends on where the commission is when the refund arrives:

- **Pending** — nothing reaches the partner's balance. A full refund marks the commission **Reversed**, a partial one **Refunded**.
- **Approved or invoiced** — the refunded share is taken back from the partner's balance: all of the commission for a full refund, and the same fraction of it for a partial refund. The balance can go negative; the partner sees it as **Balance owed**, recovered from their next commissions.
- **Already collected** — Affitor deducts it from the partner's balance, which can go negative, and sends you a notification: reconciling with the partner is a manual step.

Platform fees already billed are not refunded. A refund lowers the revenue total that counts toward your program's $10,000 fee-free tier by the refunded amount, never by more than that sale added. See [Track refund](/docs/api-reference/track-refund) for the API details.
