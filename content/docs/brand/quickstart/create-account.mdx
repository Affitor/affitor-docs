Create your Affitor brand account in about five minutes: sign up, verify your email, choose **Run a program** and name your program. Setup then walks you through the five steps to [set up your program](/docs/brand/quickstart/setup-program) and go live.

:::tip

Creating an account is free, and you don't need Stripe or payment details yet. The platform fee is $0 until your program earns its first $10,000 through affiliates, then 3.5% on affiliate-driven sales only.

:::

## Step 1: Sign up and verify your email

Your email is your sign-in and where Affitor sends account notifications.

1. Go to [affitor.com/signup](https://affitor.com/signup).
2. Sign up with your company email address and a password, or continue with Google.
3. With email sign-up, open the verification email and confirm your address.

:::tip

A shared address like `partnerships@yourcompany.com` keeps team access manageable as your program grows.

:::

## Step 2: Choose Run a program

Affitor has two sides: brands that run programs and partners who promote them. The welcome screen asks **How will you use Affitor?** Choose **Run a program**, then **Continue**. **Join programs** is the partner side.

## Step 3: Name your program and finish setup

Affitor asks for your **program name**, a **program handle** (your public page address) and your **website URL**, then opens setup. Setup has five steps: **Business**, **Commission**, **Tracking**, **Payments** and **Go live**. The logo on the Business step is optional. Everything is editable later in **Settings**; see [Set up your program](/docs/brand/quickstart/setup-program) for what each step needs and when you can go live.

## Verify it worked

Setup opens right after you name your program. When you leave it with **Save and exit**, or after **Go live**, you land on the brand dashboard.
