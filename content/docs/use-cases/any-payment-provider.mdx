```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant C as Customer
  participant W as Your site
  participant B as Your server
  participant A as Affitor
  C->>W: Opens a partner link
  W->>A: Tracker records the click
  C->>B: Signs up
  B->>A: Reports the signup with the click id and your customer key
  C->>B: Pays through your payment provider
  B->>A: Reports the sale
  A->>A: Finds the partner
  A->>A: Writes the commission
  Note over A: Approved after the hold period
  opt Refund
    B->>A: Reports the refund
    A->>A: Takes back the commission, in full or in part
  end
```

If you take payments with Stripe Checkout or Stripe subscriptions, [Sell on Stripe](/docs/use-cases/sell-on-stripe) needs no sale calls. With Stripe connected and your server reporting too, send each payment one way only: the same purchase sent both ways can count twice.
