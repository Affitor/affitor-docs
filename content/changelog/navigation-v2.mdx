Find everything in one left rail, on both the brand and the partner side of [your dashboard](https://affitor.com/brand).

Until this week, Messages, the bell and your avatar sat in the top bar, and the partner side had a layout of its own. Now both sides share one rail, with everything you use in reach.

## How it works

- **One rail**: the pages you use every day, with **Chat** among them.
- **You at the bottom**: your name opens the account menu, and the bell beside it opens your inbox.
- **Your last program**: brands switch programs from the top of the rail, and it opens the one they used last.

## The rail

**Chat** is now a rail item, next to the pages you use every day. Your name sits at the bottom of the rail: click it for your account menu and [Your account](https://affitor.com/account).

<Media src="/docs/changelog/navigation-v2/rail.png" alt="The brand left rail beside the Dashboard, from the program switcher at the top to Chat and the profile row with the bell at the bottom" />

## Your inbox

The bell beside your name opens your **Inbox** right above it. Click an event to open it, or use **Mark all as read**.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/navigation-v2/inbox.png" alt="The Inbox open above the profile row, with Mark all as read" />

## Switch programs

The program menu at the top of the rail lists your programs, with search and **Create program**. The next time you sign in on the same browser, it opens the last program you used. **Program settings** and **Billing**, and **Members** if you manage the team, open the settings rail, grouped by Program, Team, Billing and Developers.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/navigation-v2/program-menu.png" alt="The program menu open at the top of the rail, with Program settings, Members and Billing" />

## Your link on Programs

The partner side has the same layout. On **Programs**, a **Link** column shows your link for each program, with a copy button. Programs that have not approved you yet say **After approval**.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/navigation-v2/partner-link.png" alt="A partner's Programs table with a Link column and After approval for a pending program" />

## Conclusion

Brands and partners now move through Affitor the same way. [Learn more about the dashboard](https://affitor.com/docs/partners/dashboard), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
