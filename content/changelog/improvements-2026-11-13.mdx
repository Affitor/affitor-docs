### Improved

- On our public pages, the account menu now starts with **Dashboard**, which opens the side you used last.
- An account with a single side goes straight to it from **Dashboard**, even before you have opened it in this browser.
- A partly refunded commission shows the amount left, with the original crossed out, in the **Commissions** lists for brands and partners. Hover or tap it to read, for example, **Partly refunded: $40.00 of $100.00**.
- Program pages show **How you get paid** from the program's current terms: commission, attribution window, paid after and minimum payout. A **How you get paid** section typed into a program's description gives way to them, and AI assistants read the same terms.
- A commission a brand marks as fraud reads as **Rejected** when partners read their commissions, including through AI agents.
- The pricing page links to our affiliate software comparisons.
