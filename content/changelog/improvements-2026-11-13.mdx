### Improved

- On our public pages, the account menu now starts with **Dashboard**, which opens the side you used last.
- An account with a single side goes straight to it from **Dashboard**, even before you have opened it in this browser.
- If you set an **Avatar** in your account, the account button on our public pages shows it instead of your sign-in provider's photo.
- Brand and partner **Commissions** lists show a partly refunded commission's amount left, original crossed out. Hover it, or tap it as a partner, to see the sale's refunded amount.
- Program pages show **How you get paid** from the program's current terms: commission, attribution window, paid after and minimum payout.
- A **How you get paid** section typed into a program's description gives way to these terms, and AI assistants read the same terms.
- The pricing page links to our affiliate software comparisons.
- The pricing page now says there is no fee on a sale fully refunded before it is billed, and fees already billed are not refunded.
- Once a program's partner revenue reaches $10,000, the **Billing** fee hint reads 3.5% per sale after $10,000, and its tooltip says the sale that crosses it is free.
