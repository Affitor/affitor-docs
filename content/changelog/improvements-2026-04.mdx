### New

- Partners get a referral link the first time they open a program.
- Reply to a specific message in a conversation.
- The docs have CLI pages and publish `llms.txt` for AI tools.
- The docs have a changelog and a billing overdue policy.

### Improved

- Auto-approve is now the recommended option in a program's approval settings.
- The program switcher moved into the brand header.
- A program page shows Joined or Applied, so you cannot apply twice.
- Public program pages show the program's commission to visitors who are not signed in.
- New programs start from a recurring commission for 18 months and a 90-day cookie.
- Confirmations for rejecting, deleting and removing use a calmer outlined button, and deleting a partner group no longer shows an alarm box.

### Fixed

- Saving your profile no longer signs you out.
- Google sign-in lets you pick the account you want.
- The marketplace lists live programs again instead of a fixed fallback list.
