### New

- Partners get a referral link the first time they open a program.
- Reply to a specific message in a conversation.
- The docs have CLI pages, and publish `llms.txt` and `AGENTS.md` for AI agents.
- The docs have a changelog and a billing overdue policy.

### Improved

- New programs auto-approve partner applications by default.
- The brand header has a program switcher.
- A program page shows Joined or Applied, so you cannot apply twice.
- Public program pages show group commissions without signing in.
- New programs start from a recurring commission for 18 months and a 90-day cookie.
- Delete confirmations are calmer: an outlined button, no alarm box.

### Fixed

- Saving your profile no longer signs you out, and avatar upload works.
- Google sign-in lets you pick the account you want.
- The marketplace no longer fails on some requests.
