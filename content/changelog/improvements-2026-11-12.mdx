### Improved

- Brand pages carry the program in their link, and the browser tab names the page and the program, for example **Partners – Your Program – Affitor**.
- Signed in, the button at the top of our public pages reads **Dashboard**.
- We tightened sign-out after a password change, and we log less about people who open short links.

### Fixed

- A program whose commission ends after a set number of months says so, for example **12 months**, on its program page and to AI assistants; some said lifetime.
- A withdrawal in progress no longer lowers a partner's **Pending** amount on the payout page or in the **Start earning** card.
- A paused or archived program no longer takes new applications, and its invites can't be accepted until the program is live again.
