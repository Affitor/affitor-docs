### New

- **Browse by category.** Category pages list their programs with counts, sorting and page numbers.
- Partners reselling a featured offer get a referral link with click tracking, an earnings dashboard, and commissions across referral levels.
- Checkout for that offer takes more payment methods and coupon codes.

### Improved

- Category pages load faster.

### Fixed

- Balances and earnings no longer show `NaN`.
