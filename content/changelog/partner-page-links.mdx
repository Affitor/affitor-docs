Choose which pages partners can link to, and how many links each can create, in **Program settings › Partner links** at [affitor.com/brand/settings/partner-links](https://affitor.com/brand/settings/partner-links).

Until today, a partner could point a link at any page on your program's website, with no limit, and Program settings had no page for it. Now you set the allowed destinations and a limit per partner, and partners choose from your list when they create a link.

## How it works

- **Allowed destinations**: up to 20, each an HTTPS domain open to **Any page on this domain** or **One specific page**.
- **A limit per partner**: 1 to 100 additional links. The default program link doesn't count.
- **Page links**: partners link to an allowed page, then copy it or download its QR code.

## Set your allowed destinations

In **Partner links**, under **Allowed destinations**, click **Add domain**, enter an **HTTPS domain** such as `https://example.com`, and pick **Pages**. For **One specific page**, enter the **Allowed page**, for example `/pricing`. Check **I manage this domain and will ensure tracking is installed**, click **Add domain**, then **Save changes**.

## Set how many links each partner can create

Under **Additional links**, keep **Allow partners to create links to your pages** on and set **Additional links per partner**. Until you save this page, your program keeps the earlier rule: any page on your website, no limit. Owners, Admins and Members can save; Viewers can only view.

## Create a link to a page

Partners open a program, go to **Links** and click **Create link**. Add a **Private label**, keep or change the **Link key**, pick a **Domain** and enter a **Page path**. When the brand allows one specific page, the path is already filled in.

The saved link is the page with your key added, for example `https://example.com/pricing?via=k7Qm2xa` (some programs use `?aff=`). Copy it from the list, or open its QR code and click **Download PNG**. Each link now also shows **Earnings**: what it has earned, with any pending amount below it.

## Where to change it later

Change destinations or the limit on **Partner links** any time. Turning additional links off, or lowering the limit, stops new links and keeps the ones partners already share.

## Conclusion

Partners now send people to the right page, within the rules you set. [Learn more about partner links](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/partner-links), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
