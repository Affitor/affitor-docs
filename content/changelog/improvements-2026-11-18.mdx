### Improved

- A logo we find on your program's website now counts for the Launch logo requirement, so you can Launch without uploading a logo first.
- We tightened access to internal settings. Nothing changes for you.

### Fixed

- On a partner's Commissions tab, Total earnings and the earnings chart add what each row shows as paid: a fully refunded or charged-back commission adds nothing.
- The partner earnings chart now covers every commission in the current filter, not only the rows on the page you are viewing.
- A server-side sale matched by its click ID now saves the `customer_key` you send, so later sales sent with only that key match the same customer and partner.
