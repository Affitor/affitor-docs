Open [Programs](https://affitor.com/partner) to see clicks, leads, sales and earnings across every program you joined, for the date range you pick.

Until today, Programs listed your programs in a table with Clicks, Signups, Paid Signups, Revenue and Total Commission, and no totals for all of them. A program's Overview showed five numbers for 7d, 30d, 90d or All. Now one card at the top of Programs adds up every program, and each program opens on the same date range.

## How it works

- **One totals card**: Clicks, Leads, Sales and Earnings across all your programs, each against the period before.
- **One header**: on wider screens, Find programs, All programs and the date range sit next to the title.
- **Program pages**: the same four totals for one program, your top links and What you earn.

## See all your programs at once

At the top of Programs, one card shows **Clicks**, **Leads**, **Sales** and **Earnings** for every program you joined, each with its change against the previous period. Click a total to put it on the chart, where the period before is a dashed line. Below the card, **Your programs** lists each program with its clicks and earnings.

## Pick a program and a date range

Choose **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**, or pick **Start** and **End** days on the calendar. With nothing saved, Programs opens on Last 30 days. On a phone, the pickers sit under the title.

Choose a program in **All programs**, or click one in Your programs, to open its page with the same date range.

## Open a program

A program's **Overview** shows the same four totals and chart for that program alone. Below them, **Links** lists up to four of your links with their clicks, and **What you earn** shows the program's commission terms.

## Where to find it

**Programs** is in the left rail at [affitor.com/partner](https://affitor.com/partner). Old links to `/partner/analytics` open Programs.

## Conclusion

Your totals for every program and each program's numbers now share one page and one date range. [Learn more on Programs](https://affitor.com/partner), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
