### New

- Links you share from Affitor now show a preview image made for that page or program.

### Improved

- The **Integrations** page lists Grok, Muse Code, Hermes Agent and OpenClaw with the other AI agents.
- Each AI tool card on **Integrations** opens that tool's own steps in Connect your AI tool.
- The **Build on the Affitor API** tile is now **Server API**, under Frameworks and auth: track clicks, signups and sales from your backend.

### Fixed

- In **Connect an AI tool**, the partner steps now also say to pick your programs, then click **Allow**.
- Category pages that answer in Markdown, such as `/ai-video.md`, now list each program's monthly traffic.
- Requesting the same amount to the same payout account again within five minutes, while the first is pending or approved, is refused with a note pointing to **Payouts**.
- On Stripe Connect programs, a sale partly refunded before its numbers were counted now adds its net to program revenue and counts as a conversion, instead of staying at zero.
- Chat app previews of a referral short link on affitor.com no longer add to its visitor count.
