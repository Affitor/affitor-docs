### New

- Connecting an AI tool before you have a brand program now lets you allow it to **Create programs**, with **Full access** selected by default for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor.

### Improved

- When an AI tool publishes a program or reviews a partner application, it first gets a preview to show you; nothing changes until it confirms.
- The [marketplace levels](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/marketplace-levels) guide now says a logo we find on your program's website counts for Launch right away, with nothing to save.
