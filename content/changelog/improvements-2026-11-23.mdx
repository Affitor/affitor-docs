### New

- Before you have a brand program, you can now let an AI tool create programs; **Full access**, which includes it, is preselected for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor web agents.

### Improved

- When an AI tool publishes a program or reviews a partner application, it first gets a preview to show you; nothing changes until it confirms.
- The [marketplace levels](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/marketplace-levels) guide now says a logo we find on your program's website counts for Launch as soon as we find it, with nothing to save.
