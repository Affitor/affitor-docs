### Improved

- Lengthening a group's commission duration now applies to new sales only; sales that earned nothing under the old duration stay at $0.

### Fixed

- On **Partners**, **Message** in an active partner's menu or panel now opens your chat with that partner, or a new message to them if you haven't talked yet.
