### Improved

- On each program's page, partners in several programs now see only that program's commissions and customers.
- The Setting tab walks you through setup: copy your tracking snippet, which already includes your Program ID, then share your program link.
- The performance chart has a Share button that copies your program link.
- In the Partners list, each partner's revenue is now the total of all their conversions.

### Fixed

- A brand owner can no longer apply to their own program.
