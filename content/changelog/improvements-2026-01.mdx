### Improved

- Partners in several programs can filter their commissions and customers by program.
- After you create a program, a setup page shows your tracking snippet with its Program ID, ready to copy, and a step to share the program.
- The performance chart has a Share button, and revenue counts the full value of each conversion.
- The tracking script is served from the Affitor API.
