### Improved

- Chat now lives at `/brand/chat` and `/partner/chat`. Old Messages links, notification links and sign-in return links open Chat.
- Latest sales and brand commission screens fall back to your own customer key when a sale has no email, instead of Unknown customer or a numbered placeholder.
- Your name now shows in the sidebar, the account menu and Profile, instead of Brand, Partner or user@example.com. Without a name, a readable form of your email shows.
- Brand commission screens show the partner's public name, or their contact email, instead of N/A when no name is set.
- Chat shows each program by its name. Payout rows show the program instead of Unknown program, and payments without a program show their type, such as Commissions.

### Fixed

- The new message list in partner Chat only offers programs you can message. A link to one you can't message says so.
- On phones, Profile opened from the site menu no longer closes when the menu drawer closes. Commission lists say 1 result instead of 1 results.
