See what Affitor connects to on two new pages: [Integrations](https://affitor.com/integrations) and [AI agents](https://affitor.com/agents).

Until this week, this lived only in the docs. Now each has a page you can share, linked from Product in the site header and from the footer.

## How it works

- **One list**: payments, frameworks and auth, AI agents and payouts, filtered by category.
- **A page per guide**: eight integrations have a page that says what they do and sets them up in three steps.
- **AI agents**: the server address, what your agent can read, and the app you use.

## Browse integrations

Open **Integrations** from Product in the site header. Filter by Payments, Frameworks and auth, AI agents or Payouts; each tile opens its page or its guide in the docs.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/integrations-and-agents/integrations.png" alt="The Integrations page with its heading, the featured tiles, the category filter and the first row of payment integrations" />

## Open an integration

A page such as [Stripe](https://affitor.com/integrations/stripe) says what the integration does, sets it up in three steps, and links the full guide with **Read the guide**. Stripe also has **Connect Stripe**, which opens your program's Payments settings after you sign in.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/integrations-and-agents/stripe.png" alt="The Stripe integration page with Connect Stripe and Read the guide, what it does, and the three steps to connect" />

## Connect your AI app

[AI agents](https://affitor.com/agents) shows the server address to copy, what your agent can read for brands and for partners, and how to add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or your coding agent. You sign in with your Affitor account, and the connection is read-only.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/integrations-and-agents/agents.png" alt="The AI agents page with the server address and a Copy button, and what an agent can do for brands and for partners" />

## Conclusion

Everything Affitor connects to now has one place to start. [Learn more about the Affitor MCP server](https://affitor.com/docs/api-reference/mcp), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
