Your Billing page now answers three questions at a glance: what is due, what this week's invoice will look like, and where you are against the $10,000 free tier.

## Due, this week, platform fee

Three cards sit at the top of Billing. **Due** shows the amount and the date it is due. **This week** previews the next weekly invoice before it is issued. **Platform fee** shows your progress toward the free tier.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/billing-invoices-redesign/billing.png" alt="Billing: Due, This week and Platform fee cards above the invoices table, with status filters and a row menu on each invoice" />

If a program is paused for an unpaid invoice, Billing says so and tells you which invoice resumes it.

## Every invoice, easy to find

Filter invoices by status, page through older ones, download a PDF or copy an invoice number from the row menu.

## A clearer invoice page

Each invoice shows commissions, the platform fee and the total, a **By partner** table that adds up to the total, and bank transfer details with copy buttons. Workspace members can download the PDF too.
