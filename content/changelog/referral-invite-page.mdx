Your Refer a brand link now opens a page made for the person you invited.

## Who invited them

The page shows your name as the person who invited them, and the pricing up front: free until partners drive $10,000, then 3.5%. If a link is no longer active, the page says so and they can still create an account.

## Your link on the sign-in page

If you have opened your link in **Refer a brand** on this browser before, the sign-in page shows it again with **Copy**, so it is one click away the next time you sign in.
