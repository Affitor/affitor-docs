Testing your integration should never cost you anything. Sales from your Stripe test mode, your integration check or your own partner link are now marked as test data and left out of your weekly invoice.

## Voided, not pending

If an unpaid invoice contains only test sales, Affitor voids it automatically. Billing shows it as **Voided** instead of Pending, with no Pay button. If that invoice had paused your program and nothing else is open or overdue, the program resumes on its own.

## Labeled, not hidden

Test data stays visible, so your totals match what you see:

- **Test** marks your own integration tests. They are never billed and never paid out.
- **Team** marks someone on your team. Their sales wait for you to approve them as real or reject them as a test order.
