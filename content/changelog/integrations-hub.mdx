See what connects to Affitor, and how, in one diagram on the [Integrations](https://affitor.com/integrations) page.

Until this week, the Integrations page went from the four **Featured** cards straight to the full list of tiles. Now a diagram sits between them: Affitor's mark in the middle, the four groups around it, and a label on each line that says how that part connects.

## How it works

- **Four groups**: Payments, Frameworks and auth, AI agents and Payouts, named like the filters below and shown with their real logos.
- **A label per line**: **Guide**, **SDK beta**, **CLI**, **MCP** and **Built in** say how each part connects to Affitor.
- **Links**: every logo opens a page or guide, and so do **SDK beta**, **CLI** and **MCP**.

## Find your payment provider and framework

**Payments** and **Frameworks and auth** sit to the left of the Affitor mark, or above it on a phone. Payments has Stripe and Polar, each with a guide. Frameworks and auth has Next.js, Next.js with Clerk, Supabase or NextAuth, Node.js and Express, and Fastify, all through the SDK, which is in beta.

Click a logo to open its integration page, or **SDK beta** to open the Next.js guide. Above the mark, **CLI** opens the CLI quickstart, for setting up a program from the terminal.

## Find your AI app, and see how partners get paid

**AI agents** and **Payouts** sit to the right of the mark, or below it on a phone. AI agents has 12 apps that connect over MCP, from Claude and ChatGPT to Cursor and Codex. Click one to open its steps in the Connect your AI tool guide, or **MCP** to open the AI agents page.

Payouts has Wise, on a line labeled **Built in**: partners get paid by Wise or bank transfer, with nothing to connect.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/integrations-hub/agents-payouts.png" alt="Integrations diagram: the Affitor mark joined by a line labeled MCP to AI agents with 12 app logos, and by a line labeled Built in to Payouts with the Wise logo" />

## Where to find it

Open **Integrations** from **Product** in the site header, or go to [affitor.com/integrations](https://affitor.com/integrations). The diagram sits under **Featured** and above **All integrations**. The category filter changes only the list below it.

In **All integrations**, the last **Frameworks and auth** tile, which opens the API reference, is now named **API reference**. It was named **Server API**, like the payments card in **Featured**, which keeps that name.

## Conclusion

What connects to Affitor, and how, now fits in one picture. [Learn more on Integrations](https://affitor.com/integrations), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
