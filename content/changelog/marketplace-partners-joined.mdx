Each program card on the marketplace now tells you how active the program is before you open it.

## Partners joined

The footer of a card shows how many partners joined, with the initials of the program's top partners. A program nobody has joined yet says **New program**. Only initials are shown, never names or photos.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/marketplace-partners-joined/marketplace.png" alt="Marketplace cards with partner initials and counts such as 53 partners joined, New program on cards with no partners, and the Search, Newest, Filter and Refer a brand controls above them" />

## Sort and filter

- **Sort:** Newest, Highest commission, Most partners or Longest cookie.
- **Filter:** pick one or more commission types and categories, and a cookie window. Each choice shows as a chip you can remove, with **Clear** to start over.
- On a phone, Filter opens a sheet whose button shows how many programs match.

## One action per card

The right side of a card says **Apply**, shows your status if you already applied or joined, or says **Your program** on a program you run.
