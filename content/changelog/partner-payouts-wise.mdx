Get paid through Wise or a bank transfer, from one short setup in [Your account › Payouts](https://affitor.com/account/payouts).

Until this week, partners could withdraw without a legal name or a tax form on file. Now you finish three steps once, and then you can withdraw any amount from $20.

## How it works

- **Set up once**: a legal name, a payout method and a tax form, all in one card.
- **Wise or bank**: Affitor sends your money through Wise, to a Wise account or to a bank account in your name.
- **From $20**: once the card says **Ready**, withdraw any amount from $20.

## Open Payouts

Open [Your account › Payouts](https://affitor.com/account/payouts). The **Get ready to be paid** card lists what is still missing.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/partner-payouts-wise/setup.png" alt="The Get ready to be paid card with three rows: legal name, payout method and tax form" />

## Add your legal name and payout method

Add your **legal name** as on your bank account and ID. It goes on your tax form and payout records. Then choose **Add payout method**: a Wise account, or a bank account in your legal name.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/partner-payouts-wise/method.png" alt="The Add payout method dialog with recipient country, currency and Wise as the type" />

## Fill in your tax form

Say whether you are a US citizen or US tax resident. If you are not, sign a W-8BEN as a person or a W-8BEN-E as a business, right in the app. If you are, declare a W-9, and our accountant emails it to you to sign. Affitor never asks for your SSN or EIN, and brands never see your tax form.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/partner-payouts-wise/tax-form.png" alt="The tax form panel answered No, with A person · W-8BEN picked: country of tax residence, date of birth, permanent address and an optional foreign tax number" />

## Withdraw

When all three steps are done, the card shows **Ready**. Withdraw from your payout page as before. If a step is still missing, the page says **Finish payout setup first** and takes you there with **Go to Payouts**.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/partner-payouts-wise/withdraw.png" alt="The Withdraw page with the available amount, the $20 minimum and a prompt to finish payout setup" />

## Manage your details

Change your payout method or legal name in Payouts at any time; a new legal name needs a new tax form. A W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E stays valid until December 31 of the third year after you sign it, and then the card asks you to sign again.

PayPal is no longer offered. A PayPal method you saved before stays visible so you can remove it.

## Conclusion

Partners now get paid through Wise or a bank transfer, from one setup they do once. [Learn more about payouts](https://affitor.com/docs/partners/payouts), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
