### Improved

- **Chat** groups messages under a centered day and time at each new day or after an hour of quiet. Times show on hover or tap; only brands see writer names.
- You type a new password once, in one **New password** field, on **Login and security** in your account and on the **Set a new password** page.
- Clicks on short links count only people who open them: crawlers, link previews, uptime monitors and browser prefetches no longer add clicks.

### Fixed

- In the **Status** column of a partner's **Programs** list, a joined program that is paused shows **Paused**, not **Active**.
- A sign-up with a password keeps **Check Your Email** on screen after **Create account**, with the address you entered; sign-ups by email code or Google never show it.
- A password chosen at sign-up stops working when you confirm by the email link: sign in with **Email me a code** or set a new password. Confirmed accounts are unchanged.
- A program page shows the **Application** checklist only while you are signed in; once your sign-in has expired it no longer appears.
- Only a program's **Owners** and **Admins** can change its attribution window and hold period. **Members** see both as read-only.
- Commission lists also show $0 commissions for sales outside a program's terms, and brands can't **Approve**, **Reject**, **Extend Hold** or **Mark as Fraud** them.
- Column headers in the partner **Programs** table stay on one line.
- Text fields you can't edit right now, such as a form that is saving, look disabled.
