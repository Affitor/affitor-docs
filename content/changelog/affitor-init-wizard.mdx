The CLI now signs you in through your browser, so programs you create belong to your dashboard account. `programs` lists them and `whoami` shows who is signed in.

```bash
npx affitor login
npx affitor init
```

In a Next.js project, `init` detects the framework, installs the SDK and adds the tracking code. It asks before installing anything and shows a diff of the change to your app layout before applying it.

A server-side Node SDK ships alongside it, so you can record sales from your backend. Since June 2026 the SDK is published as `@affitor/sdk`.
