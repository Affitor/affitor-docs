### Improved

- In **Connect an AI tool**, the **Other** tab now has steps for Grok: add Affitor as a custom connector at grok.com/connectors, then sign in.

### Fixed

- Deleting an API key works again. After you type the key's name to confirm, the key is deleted instead of returning an error.
- On the AI app consent screen, **Cancel** now works for accounts that are not yet a brand or a partner, and takes you back to your AI tool.
