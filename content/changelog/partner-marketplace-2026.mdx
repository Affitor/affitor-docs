Partners can find their next program without leaving Affitor. The marketplace lists live programs with search that forgives typos, filters for programs you have joined, applied to, or not yet joined, a commission type filter, and sorting by commission.

The marketplace has been redesigned since; [browse it today](https://affitor.com/marketplace).
