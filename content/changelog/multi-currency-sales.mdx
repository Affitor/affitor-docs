Send sales in other currencies, and send the tax inside each sale. Starting Thursday, November 5, 2026 (16:00 UTC), we handle tax and currency differently in commissions and platform fees on new sales.

Until now, a sale sent through the API was commissioned on the amount as sent: amounts were not converted between currencies, and `tax_cents` was accepted but not used. Now the currency and the tax both count.

## How it works

- Commissions and platform fees are calculated on the price your customer paid after discounts and before tax, whenever your payment provider or your API call gives us the tax amount.
- Sales in other currencies are converted to USD at the latest daily exchange rate when the sale is recorded, and we keep the original amount next to it.
- Commissions already recorded do not change.

## Send the tax with each sale

If your amount includes tax, add the tax in the `tax_cents` field of `POST /api/v1/track/sale`, in the same unit as `amount_cents`. Set `currency` to the sale's own currency:

```json tab="Request body"
{
  "transaction_id": "txn_abc123",
  "customer_key": "usr_9876",
  "amount_cents": 490000,
  "tax_cents": 40000,
  "currency": "VND"
}
```

Despite its name, `amount_cents` uses the currency's minor unit, not always hundredths: USD 49.00 is `4900`, VND 490,000 is `490000`, and BHD 1.234 is `1234`. A currency we do not support returns `422 unsupported_currency` and creates no sale.

## What to do

If you report refunds, send `refund_amount_cents` in the sale's own currency and minor unit, or leave it out to refund the whole sale. A value of 0 is no longer accepted for sales from November 5: `0`, a negative number or a non-integer returns `400 invalid_request`. Refunds of earlier sales work as before.

```bash tab="cURL"
curl -X POST https://api.affitor.com/api/v1/track/refund \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_PROGRAM_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"transaction_id":"txn_abc123","refund_amount_cents":10000,"refund_id":"re_123"}'
```

## Conclusion

From November 5, tax and currency count in commissions and platform fees on new sales. [Learn more in Track Sale](https://affitor.com/docs/api-reference/track-sale) and [Track Refund](https://affitor.com/docs/api-reference/track-refund), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
