### New

- The tracker is on npm as `@affitor/tracker` (since replaced by `@affitor/sdk`), and calls made before it loads are queued instead of lost.
- When the browser cookie is missing, a sale can still be credited to the right partner by matching the customer's user ID or email.
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages.

### Improved

- **A redesigned homepage**, with how it works, the features, a dashboard preview, pricing and an FAQ.
- The free YouTube transcript tool was rebuilt, and transcripts now show timestamps.
- Partial refunds are tracked and reduce the commission in proportion to the amount refunded.
- Customer emails are masked in the brand's customer list.
- On each program page, the Balance card shows your actual balance, and commission totals include approved commissions.
- The setup page shows whether tracking is verified.
- Program pages in the directory can be read by search engines, with sharing previews. These pages now open the marketplace.
- A simpler 404 page with Go back and Go home buttons.
- Pages load faster.
- The docs have a sitemap and `llms.txt`.

### Fixed

- A brand owner can no longer apply to their own program.
- Logging out returns you to the homepage.
- The Categories page and the Show more button on the program list work again (both have since moved to the marketplace).
