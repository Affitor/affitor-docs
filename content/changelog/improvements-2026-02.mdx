### New

- The tracker is on npm as `@affitor/tracker`, and calls made before it loads are queued instead of lost.
- When the browser cookie is missing, a sale can still be credited to the right partner by matching the customer's user ID or email.
- A free YouTube transcript tool: paste a video link and read the transcript with timestamps.
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages.

### Improved

- **A redesigned homepage**, with how it works, the features, a live dashboard preview, a pricing calculator and an FAQ.
- Partial refunds are tracked and reduce the commission by the refunded amount.
- Customer emails are masked in the brand's customer list.
- The partner dashboard shows your balance and a commission summary.
- The setup page shows whether tracking is verified.
- Program pages in the directory can be read by search engines, with sharing previews.
- A helpful 404 page with search.
- Pages load faster.
- The docs have a sitemap and `llms.txt`.

### Fixed

- A brand owner can no longer apply to their own program.
- Logging out returns you to the homepage.
- The Categories page and the Show more button on program lists work again.
