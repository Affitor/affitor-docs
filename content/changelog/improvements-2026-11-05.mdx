### Improved

- If you are on a brand team and also a partner, Chat keeps each side's conversations, people, replies and uploads apart when you switch sides.
- Partners and Groups show the rows you last saw right away when you come back, then refresh them in place.
- Commissions refreshes its list and totals when you come back and after every action. Totals turn grey while they update.

### Fixed

- Bulk Reject on Commissions sends only the rows selected in the current list, and cancels if any of them stopped being pending.
- When the list fails to load, Commissions shows the error alert alone, without an empty "No commissions found" table.
- Switching programs on Groups clears the open group, so its details never show the previous program's partners.
