### Improved

- Continue on the first step of `/brand/new` now brings in your website's logo as the program logo when a large enough icon exists. A logo you already have stays.
- Chat shows a partner's public name, or a dash when none is set, instead of an email prefix or a word like Partner.
- The Sources report labels partners by their public name, or a dash when none is set, instead of an account username or a partner ID.
- Viewers on a brand team can read Chat but not send: the composer, Reply and New message are hidden, also when they open a link directly.
- After you send a message, the message box keeps your cursor so you can type the next one.
- When you approve or reject an application outside the Inbox, its notification shows Resolved right away instead of still offering Review application.

### Fixed

- You can attach images and PDFs again in existing Chat conversations. The first message in a new conversation stays text only.
- Your Profile and a program's Customers tab show a dash instead of N/A for missing dates and names. A canceled plan is labeled in gray instead of red.
