# Affitor context for AI-made posts and videos

Use only what is in this file and in the entry you are working from (its page, its `.md` and its `context`). If a number, URL, button label, name or claim is not there, leave it out. Updated Oct 17, 2026.

## Brand
- Name: Affitor.
- Logo: always the files, never redrawn or typed. (The live site's nav shows the name as typed text; do not copy that.)
  - Mark: https://affitor.com/blog/logos/affitor-icon-blue-on-white.svg (PNG: https://affitor.com/brand/affitor-icon-512.png), the blue "A" glyph on white. Use it as is: no colored square or circle around it, and never a font "A" in its place.
  - Wordmark: https://affitor.com/docs/affitor-logo.svg, the word "Affitor" drawn as shapes in #155DFC. Never re-type the name in a font in its place.
- Colors: brand #155DFC (buttons, links) · ink #262626 (titles) · text #3C4257 · muted #6A7383 · lines #E3E8EE · soft background #F8F9FA. Light backgrounds first.
- Type: Inter; titles weight 600.
- Screens: use the real screenshots, raw crops and loops listed in the entry (demo data). Do not redraw or invent product screens. To show a screen the entry does not have, re-record it from the entry's `.steps.json` or ask for a capture.

## Facts that hold everywhere
- Pricing: free until a program earns its first $10,000 in affiliate revenue, then 3.5% of affiliate-driven revenue. No subscription.
- Refer a brand link (Affitor's own referral program): affitor.com/r/<username>. The username is chosen in Account › Profile.
- A partner's link for a brand's program goes to the brand's own site: `<brand site>/r/<code>` when the brand set that up, otherwise `<page>?aff=<code>`. It is not an affitor.com link.
- Partner payouts: Wise or bank transfer.
- AI agents: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and coding agents connect to Affitor over MCP at https://mcp.affitor.com/mcp, signing in with an Affitor account. Read-only.
- Payments: brands connect Stripe in one click, or send each sale from their server.

## Never say
- A setup time ("in under an hour", "in minutes") or a home-page tagline ("Pay only for results", "Zero upfront"), unless the entry quotes it. Those lines belong to the home page (its hero, stats row and sections); they are not an entry's facts.
- That Affitor imports partners or history from another tool.
- PayPal payouts.
- Customer or partner names, logos, numbers or quotes that are not in the entry. Use the demo names in the entry's media.
- A comparison with another tool without the date it was checked.
- A URL or button label that is not in the entry or in this file.
