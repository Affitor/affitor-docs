### Fixed

- A Stripe sale that was recorded but whose commission was not created now gets its commission, once. This happens when Stripe sends the sale again, or at our daily check for paid sales from the last 7 days that were not refunded or disputed.
- When an approved commission is partly refunded before it is invoiced, the weekly invoice now bills the part the partner keeps. Until now that part was left off the invoice. The sale's platform fee is billed in full, as before.
