### Fixed

- A recorded Stripe sale missing its commission now gets it once, on Stripe's resend or our daily check of the last 7 days' paid sales with no refund or dispute.
- An approved commission partly refunded before invoicing is now billed on the weekly invoice: the part the partner keeps, plus the sale's full platform fee. Until now neither was billed.
- The weekly invoice is now written in one step: if any line fails, nothing is written, and the next run bills each commission once.
