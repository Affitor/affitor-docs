### New

- **Filter programs** by industry, commission type, recurring or one-time payouts, and payment method. Filters are saved in the page link, and one button clears them.
- Program pages show traffic by country.
- Each program shows the average value of a conversion and its recurring commission.
- Each program has a short AI summary.
- Brands and partners can submit a program to be listed.
- New accounts start on a free plan.

### Improved

- Right-click or Cmd-click opens a program in a new tab.
- Lists show a loading outline instead of a blank page.
