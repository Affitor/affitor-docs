### New

- Sign in with your Google account.
- **Filter programs** by industry, commission rate, one-time or lifetime commission, and payment method. Filters are saved in the page link, and one button clears them.
- Pick which table columns you see; Affitor remembers your choice.
- Program pages show traffic by country.
- Each program shows the average value of a conversion and its recurring commission.
- Each program has a short AI summary.
- Brands and partners can submit a program to be listed.

### Improved

- Right-click a program to open it in a new tab.

These features were part of the program directory, which became the Affitor Marketplace in June 2026.
