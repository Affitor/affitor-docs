Add a payout method and withdraw without leaving your [Payout page](https://affitor.com/partner/payout).

Until this week, **Withdraw** on the Payout page took you to a separate page. Now it opens a short form on top of the Payout page, and adding a payout method works the same way.

## How it works

- **Your balance**: **Ready to withdraw**, **Pending** and **Total paid** sit at the top.
- **Your method**: with no payout method saved, the button reads **Add payout method**. With one saved, it reads **Withdraw**.
- **Your history**: search it, filter it and export it from **Payout history**.

## Add a payout method

With no payout method yet, **Add payout method** opens a form on the page. Choose Wise or a bank account and save it. The button then changes to **Withdraw**.

## Withdraw

**Withdraw** opens a form with the amount, which starts at everything ready, the payout method it goes to, the **Fee** and **You receive**. Confirm, and the page confirms your withdrawal. If your balance is under the minimum, the page says so.

<Media light="/docs/changelog/partner-payout-page/withdraw-light.png" dark="/docs/changelog/partner-payout-page/withdraw-dark.png" alt="The Withdraw form with the amount, the payout method, the fee and what you receive" />

## Your payout history

**Payout history** lists your payouts ten at a time. Use **Search payouts**, open **Filters** to pick a status or a type, and **Export** the rows on the page as a CSV file. Click a row for its details.

<Media light="/docs/changelog/partner-payout-page/history-light.png" dark="/docs/changelog/partner-payout-page/history-dark.png" alt="Payout history with search, Filters and Export above the table, and the page controls below" />

## Manage your saved methods

Edit or remove a saved payout method in [Payouts](https://affitor.com/account/payouts) in your account settings.

## Conclusion

Your balance, your payout method and your history now live on one page. [Learn more about getting paid](https://affitor.com/docs/partners/payouts), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
