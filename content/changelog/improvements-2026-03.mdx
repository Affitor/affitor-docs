### New

- **Payouts.** Partners see a transaction history with a status timeline and can request a withdrawal of their approved balance.
- **Team members.** Invite teammates to your workspace and manage them.
- Set a hold period and a cookie window per program. Partners see both on the program.
- A full customer refund now marks the commission reversed, and a partial refund marks it refunded. Commission filters and counts include reversed.
- Change your password from your profile, and show or hide your password while signing in.
- The tracker shares attribution across your subdomains.

### Improved

- Move partners between groups in bulk, and share your program link from the Partners page.
- The brand dashboard is redesigned: trend cards, a new chart, an Action Center and a Getting Started checklist.
- A commission shows its fee and revenue breakdown.
- The docs have a 3-step integration guide and a guide to testing your integration.
- A new Affitor logo across the site and the docs.
- Customer tables can be sorted and filtered by status.
- Search tolerates typos on the partner customer list and marketplace, brand messages, and partner groups.
- Partner program pages work on phones, and link destinations are checked before saving.
- Server-side lead examples on the setup page use the `/api/v1/track/lead` path.

### Security

- Sign-in sessions are kept in secure cookies.
