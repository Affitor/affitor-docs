### New

- **Payouts.** Brands record a payout to partners, and partners see a transaction history with a status timeline.
- **Team members.** Invite teammates to your workspace and manage them, with an activity log.
- Set a hold period and a cookie window per program. Partners see both on the program.
- Commissions can be marked reversed, and the filters count them.
- Change your password, with a show and hide toggle.
- The tracker shares attribution across your subdomains.

### Improved

- The brand dashboard is redesigned: trend cards, a new chart, an Action Center and a Getting Started checklist.
- A commission shows its fee and revenue breakdown and an audit trail.
- The docs have a Quickstart and a guide to testing your integration.
- A new Affitor logo across the site and the docs.
- Customer tables can be sorted and filtered by status.
- Search tolerates typos on partner pages, messages and partner groups.
- Partner program pages work on phones, and link destinations are checked before saving.
- Lead tracking uses the `/api/v1/track/lead` path.

### Security

- Sign-in sessions are kept in secure cookies.
