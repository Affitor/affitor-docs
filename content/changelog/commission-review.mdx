date: 2026-02-24
type: feature
audience: [brands]
surfaces: [dashboard]
description: Review partner commissions in the dashboard, approve or reject them with a reason, act on many at once, and mark approved ones as paid.

Brands can now review partner commissions in the dashboard before they are paid. Approve a commission, or reject it with a reason. Select many commissions to act on them at once, then mark approved commissions as paid.
