The marketplace now shows every program as a card with the numbers that matter: the commission and how long it pays, the cookie length, and how soon you get paid.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/marketplace-redesign/marketplace.png" alt="The marketplace: a featured program on top, then search, type filters, a category menu, sort, and a grid of program cards" />

## Find the right program faster

Search by name or description, filter by Recurring, One-time or Per action, pick a category, and sort the list. Each filter shows how many programs it holds. Every filter lives in the URL, so you can share a view.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/marketplace-redesign/filter.mp4" alt="Switching between All, Recurring and One-time programs" />

Programs listed in the last two weeks carry a **New** pill. When you are signed in, programs you have joined or applied to say **Joined** or **Applied**. On the full list, the Affitor Refer Program sits first, so you can always earn by introducing a brand to Affitor.

## Terms at a glance

Each program page opens with four numbers: commission, cookie length, when you get paid, and the minimum payout. Below them are the brand's About section when it has one, an FAQ and similar programs in the same category. Applying takes one optional field.
