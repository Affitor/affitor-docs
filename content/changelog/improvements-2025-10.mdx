### New

- A public application form for brands that want to run an affiliate program on Affitor.
- Brands can filter the customers their partners referred by status: lead, conversion or click.
- Commissions are now created on each subscription renewal, not only on the first payment.

### Fixed

- The revenue chart no longer breaks when there is no revenue yet.
