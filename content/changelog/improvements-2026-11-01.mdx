### Improved

- On Referrals, referred brands and invite pages, program logos use the uploaded logo, or else the website logo, shown whole in a white framed tile.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/improvements-2026-11-01/logos.png" alt="Referrals with program logos framed in white tiles next to a program without a logo shown by its initial" />

### For developers

- `POST /api/v1/track/lead` accepts a positive whole number as `customer_key`, and `POST /api/v1/track/refund` accepts one as `transaction_id` or `refund_id`, treated like the same value sent as a string.
