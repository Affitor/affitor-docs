Partners get a proper Settings page. **Account** holds your name, country, website, social handles and avatar. **Payout** holds where you get paid. **Privacy & Security** is where you change your password.

Payout details cover bank transfer, Wise and PayPal, and you can request a withdrawal from the Payout page. Your avatar shows up across the dashboard as soon as you upload it.

If you've been emailing support to update partner details, that's no longer needed. [Log in and open Settings to update your profile →](https://affitor.com/welcome)
