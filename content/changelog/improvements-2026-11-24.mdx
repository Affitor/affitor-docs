### Improved

- We email you about an invoice 3 days before it is due (when it was issued by then), on the due date, and 3 and 7 days after.
- An unpaid program now pauses 10 days after the due date instead of on it, and only after one of those emails has told you the pause date.
- While a program is paused for an unpaid invoice, its tracking links now keep counting clicks.
- Program pages with at least 20 words of About and commission terms can now be found on Google and are listed in the sitemap, down from 150 words.
