### New

- Send feedback from the account menu: pick a topic (Idea, Bug, Question or Other), write a note, and optionally rate how Affitor feels. The team reads every message.

### Improved

- Dashboard analytics no longer stop at "Building history for this range": cards and charts load your numbers for every range in the date picker.
- Last 30 days now includes today: it covers the past 29 days plus today so far.
- The browser tab icon, the AI app consent page and the icon MCP clients display all use the blue Affitor mark from the site header.

### For developers

- You can add Affitor to Grok on grok.com as a custom connector with `https://mcp.affitor.com/mcp`, then sign in and approve it on Affitor.
