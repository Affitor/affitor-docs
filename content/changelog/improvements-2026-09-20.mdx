### New

- **Forgot your password?** The sign-in page now has forgot-password and reset-password pages, so you can get back into your account on your own.

### Improved

- Clicking a notification opens the page it is about.
- Emails from Affitor now come from Affitor, with a plain-text version and a reply-to address.
- Program descriptions are drafted from the brand's website, with a fallback when the site cannot be read.
- Sign-in, marketplace and dashboard pages load faster and shift less while they load.
- Marketplace program pages include the commission in the page itself, and every live program is in the sitemap, so search engines and AI assistants can read your terms.
- `llms.txt` and the Markdown version of each public page are generated correctly for agents.
- The public marketplace API returns each program's `documentId` and default commission policy.
- The homepage links to the Marketplace from the top bar and the footer.

### Fixed

- Brands no longer see Approve and Decline on notifications for partners who already joined.
- One-time programs show an average sale value without "/month".
- Partners see only the commission terms that are active right now.
- A completed payout amount can no longer change after it is paid.
- Message drafts are kept, and a chat message that failed to send is recovered.
- Click tracking matches a partner's `?aff=` code to the right partner.
- No hold-extension notification for a commission worth $0.
- Featured programs stay featured.
- The brand profile explains why it could not load instead of redirecting you away.
- The homepage pricing states the $10,000 free tier.

### Security

- We tightened how chat attachments, account balances and public marketplace data are handled.
