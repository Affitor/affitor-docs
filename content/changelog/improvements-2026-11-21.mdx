### New

- A partner's connected AI app can apply to a Launched program for them. The partner gets one in-app notice for each application it sends.

### Improved

- Pages outside the partner area download about 88 KB less code on a first visit: analytics starts after the page paints, and partner code loads only in the partner area.
- The brand home loads its summary cards in one request, and starts its program list together with the user check.
- The tracking check in brand setup stops asking for new clicks while its tab is hidden, and checks again when you return.
