We now run affiliate programs. Brands set up a program, partners join it, and we track what each partner brings in.

- **For brands:** sign up as a brand with your company email, create programs with your logo, set commission tiers with a percentage rate that can step up with the number of referrals, and auto-accept partner applications. A summary of your programs, partners and sales.
- **For partners:** apply to a program, get a tracked referral link with a name you choose, and see the customers you referred.
- **Tracking:** add the tracking script to your site to record clicks and sign-ups. Sales were recorded when buyers paid through an Affitor checkout link, which we later retired.

Applying twice to the same program is blocked.
