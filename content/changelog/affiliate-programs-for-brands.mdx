Affitor is now an affiliate platform. Brands set up a program, partners join it, and Affitor tracks what each partner brings in.

- **For brands:** a brand profile, programs with your logo, flat or percentage commission tiers, and auto-accept for partner applications. A summary of your programs, partners and sales.
- **For partners:** apply to a program, get a tracked referral link with a name you choose, and see the customers you referred, your balance and which commissions are paid.
- **Tracking:** add the tracking script to your site, and clicks, leads and sales are recorded and attributed to the right partner.

Applying twice to the same program is blocked.
