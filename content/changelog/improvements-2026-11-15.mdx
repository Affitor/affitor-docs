### Improved

- When the partner is the buyer (their email matches the customer's), a new commission now waits for the brand instead of being approved on its own. Its row says so: approve it if it was a real purchase, or reject it.
- A sale sent through the Tracking API with **test** or **e2e** as a word in its order reference also waits for the brand, with a line asking to approve it if it was a real customer or reject it as a test.
- Partners see these commissions as under brand review.
- The **Payout sent** notice shows the amount you receive, in your payout's currency, and says the money arrives on Wise's usual timing (or your bank's, or PayPal's).

### Fixed

- Invoice PDFs no longer print empty wire transfer lines, and invoice emails ask you to pay by wire transfer only when it is set up. Otherwise they send you to the invoice page to pay.
