### New

- Record refunds through the tracking API, so the matching commission is reversed.
- Notifications and billing are scoped per program for brands with several programs.
- A quick start popover in the top bar, and Billing as its own tab.
- A program handle field in program settings and when you create a program.
- Withdraw an application from your Programs list.

### Improved

- App addresses are shorter (`/brand/...`, `/partner/...`), and "advertiser" is now "brand" everywhere.
- Settings, forms, charts, status pills and alerts share one calmer design.
- Apply to a program first and sign up after: your application continues once you are in.
- The attribution window is one setting, and it is applied when sales settle. Chargebacks reverse commissions.
- The partner table sorts by most clicks first and remembers your sort.
- The SDK is published as `@affitor/sdk`, and the docs use it everywhere.
- **list.affitor.com is retired** and now opens the marketplace.
- Programs without a logo get a default gradient logo, and SVG logos upload.
- PayPal is no longer a withdrawal method.

### Fixed

- Logging out clears your session completely.
- Program pages no longer show default commission numbers that the brand never set.
- The tracking setup wizard shows the right snippet and refund guidance.
- Test events stay out of brand metrics.
- Partner chat no longer shows a message twice.

### Security

- We tightened access between accounts and now store API tokens hashed.
