You no longer wait for us to publish your program. Once setup is complete (tracking script installed, leads tracked, Stripe connected and a first real sale recorded), the program goes live by itself and shows in the marketplace for partners to find.

In June 2026 this became an explicit Go Live button, so you choose the moment.
