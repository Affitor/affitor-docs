Find what you need in the [docs](https://affitor.com/docs) by the job you want done.

Until this week, the docs opened by section, with no search page and no walk-through of a whole setup. Now they open by job, with a Help Center and use cases.

## How it works

- **By job**: choose **Brands** or **Partners**, and pages are grouped by task.
- **Help Center**: search the common questions in one place.
- **Use cases**: a whole setup, with each guide in order.

## Choose Brands or Partners

The switch at the top of the docs shows the pages for your side, grouped by the job you want done.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/help-center-use-cases/nav.png" alt="The Affitor docs home with a Brands and Partners switch above the guides" />

## Search the Help Center

The [Help Center](https://affitor.com/docs/help) searches the common questions, by category. If you are still stuck, it links to support.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/help-center-use-cases/help.png" alt="The Help center page with a search box, category filters and cards for short answers" />

## Follow a use case

[Use cases](https://affitor.com/docs/use-cases) walk through a whole setup, from a partner's link to a commission: selling on Stripe, any payment provider, and subscriptions. A new guide, [Connect a new Stripe account](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/tracking/connect-stripe-account), covers creating the account and connecting it in Program settings › Payments.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/help-center-use-cases/use-cases.png" alt="The Use cases page with three cards, each showing its flow from a partner link to a commission" />

## Copy a page for your AI

Every docs page has **Copy for LLM** and **View as Markdown** in its header, so you can hand a page to your coding agent as it is.

## Conclusion

The docs now start from what you want to do. [Learn more in the Help Center](https://affitor.com/docs/help), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
