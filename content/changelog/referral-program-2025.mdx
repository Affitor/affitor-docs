Partners can now earn by sharing Affitor. A short onboarding sets you up as a referrer, and you create your own referral links to Affitor's pages. The referral dashboard shows how they perform.

- Visitors, leads and conversions at a glance, with a performance chart.
- An activity feed of what happened with your links.
- A list of the commissions you earned.
