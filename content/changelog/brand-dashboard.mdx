Open [your Dashboard](https://affitor.com/brand) to see clicks, leads, sales and revenue for the date range you pick, each against the period before it.

Until today, the Dashboard showed Performance Overview: five totals, one chart with no comparison line, a choice of the last 7, 30 or 90 days or All time, and an Action Center for pending applications. Now every total is compared with the period before, and your latest sales, top partners and what needs you sit below.

## How it works

- **Four totals**: Clicks, Leads, Sales and Revenue, each with its change against the previous period.
- **One chart**: the total you pick, with the previous period as a dashed line.
- **Three lists**: Latest sales, Top partners, and Needs you when something waits on you.

## Pick a total and a date range

Click **Clicks**, **Leads**, **Sales** or **Revenue** to put it on the chart. Above it, choose **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**, or pick **Start** and **End** days on the calendar.

The chart draws your range as a solid line and the period before it as a lighter dashed line. Hover a point to compare the two. The Dashboard remembers your total and range in this browser.

## See sales, partners and what needs you

**Latest sales** lists your most recent sales with their amounts. **Top partners** lists the partners who brought in the most revenue in your range. Rows open Commissions or Partners, and **View all** opens the full page.

**Needs you** lists what waits on you: applications to review, and open invoices, shown as **Pay partners by** a date with the amount. With nothing waiting, it stays hidden.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/brand-dashboard/lists.png" alt="Latest sales and Top partners, each with View all, and Needs you below with applications to review and Pay partners by a date with the amount" />

## Cards keep their size

The totals, the chart and both lists keep the same size while they load, when they are empty and when they have data. Empty lists say so, such as **No sales yet**, and a list or the chart that cannot load offers **Try again**.

## Where to find it

**Dashboard** is in the left rail at [affitor.com/brand](https://affitor.com/brand) and shows the program you have open.

## Conclusion

Your totals, the period before and what needs you now sit on one page. [Learn more on your Dashboard](https://affitor.com/brand), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
