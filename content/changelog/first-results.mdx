See your first results as they happen: brands on [Get started](https://affitor.com/brand/get-started), partners on [Programs](https://affitor.com/partner).

Until this week, setup progress showed up in several places, and nothing told you when your program or your link first worked. Now each side has one card that marks its first results, with one next step.

## How it works

- **Pick your side first**: sign-up asks brand or partner before the form.
- **Brands**: four first results on Get started, once the program is live.
- **Partners**: three first results on Programs, each with its date.

## Pick your side

[Sign-up](https://affitor.com/signup) asks **I'm a brand** or **I'm a partner** before the form, and the next screen has your answer already picked.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/first-results/signup.png" alt="The sign-up choice with two cards: I'm a brand and I'm a partner" />

## First results, for brands

Once your program is live, **First results** on Get started tracks four steps: **Your first partner joined**, **A partner sent you traffic**, **Your first sale from a partner** and **You paid your first partner**. The next step to reach shows what to do, with a button such as **Share program** or **Go to commissions**. Before your program is live, the card says these start once it is.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/first-results/brand.png" alt="The First results card on a brand's Get started page, with the first partner joined and Share program as the next step" />

## Start earning, for partners

Once your partner setup is done, **Start earning** at the top of Programs tracks three steps: **Someone clicked your link**, **You earned your first commission** and **You got paid**, each with the date it happened. If money is waiting and you have no payout method, the card asks you to add one. Use **Hide this list** when you no longer need it.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/first-results/partner.png" alt="The Start earning card on a partner's Programs page, with the first click reached and the first commission as the next step" />

## Conclusion

Brands and partners now see the moment their program or their link starts to work. [Learn more about getting started](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/quickstart), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
