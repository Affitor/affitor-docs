Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another AI tool to several of your programs in one step, or to all of them, including programs you join later.

Until this week, a brand connection reached one program: the consent screen asked you to pick a single **Program**, so each program needed its own connection, and a partner connection always covered every program you had joined. Now you choose the programs on the same screen, and one connection reads all of them.

## How it works

- **Pick programs**: tick up to 50 programs, or choose **All current and future programs**.
- **Read-only**: a connection reads your data and changes nothing.
- **One list to manage**: **AI agents** shows which programs each connection reaches.

## Choose programs when you connect

When your AI tool asks to connect, Affitor shows the account you are signed in with, then **Access** and **Programs**. Open **Read account data**, always on, to see the four kinds of data the tool can read.

Under **Programs**, tick the programs you want, each shown with your role, or choose **All current and future programs** so programs you join later are included without reconnecting. With a long list, **Search programs** narrows it. If you are on a brand team and also a partner, switch between **As a brand** and **As a partner**. Then click **Allow**.

## See and remove access in Settings

Brands open **AI agents** under **Developers** in Program settings. **Connections** lists each AI tool with **Connected by**, **Added**, **Last used** and **Access**, and shows the programs it reaches under its name. Partners find their list under **Integrations** in the account menu.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/mcp-multiple-programs/connections.png" alt="AI agents in Program settings with two connections, one listing its programs and one with All current and future programs" />

**Disconnect** your own connection to end its access to every program in it. Removing a teammate's connection from a program ends its access to that program only; its other programs keep their access.

## Conclusion

One connection now reaches the programs you choose. [Learn more in AI agents](https://affitor.com/brand/settings/ai-agents), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
