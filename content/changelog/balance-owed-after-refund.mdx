Sometimes a customer asks for a refund after you have already withdrawn the commission for that sale. Until now, that amount quietly disappeared from your history. Now your payout page shows it.

## Balance owed

Under **Ready to withdraw**, a **Balance owed** line shows the total. It is recovered from your next commissions, and the line goes away once it is paid down.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/balance-owed-after-refund/payout.png" alt="The partner payout page with a Balance owed line under Ready to withdraw" />

## How it is recovered

New commissions pay the balance down first. You can withdraw again once your available balance is above zero. If a customer is refunded again later for a larger amount, the rest of that commission is reversed the same way.

If a refund leaves your balance too low to cover a withdrawal you requested but have not received, that withdrawal is reduced or canceled to match.

Most partners will never see this line. It appears only after a refund on money you had already withdrawn.
