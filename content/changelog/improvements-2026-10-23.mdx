### Improved

- Members shows Name, Permission level and Status, with a Permission level dropdown per member you can change. Make owner lets an Owner add a co-owner and stay an owner.
- Commissions shows Revenue, Commission and To pay for the selected date range. Status and search filter only the table below.
- A notification for an application waiting on you has a Review application button that opens Partners › Applied, where you approve or decline. Partners has one Share program button.
- On your own program's marketplace page, Your program opens that program's Dashboard.
- When a program is archived, its partners can't create links or change destinations, its page offers a way back to Programs, and its links stop recording clicks.

### Fixed

- The Applied tab count on Partners updates right after you approve or decline an application.

### For developers

- Tracking a click on an archived program's link with `POST /api/v1/track/click` returns `410` with error code `link_disabled`.
