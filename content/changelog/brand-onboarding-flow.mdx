Set up a new program in one full-screen flow, one step at a time, at [affitor.com/brand/new](https://affitor.com/brand/new).

Until today, creating a program started on a separate form that also asked for a program handle, and the setup steps after it sat next to a step list and a preview of what partners see, with a Skip for now button on Tracking and Payments. Now every step is one screen in the same flow.

## How it works

- **Five steps**: Business, Commission, Tracking, Payments and Go live, each with **Step n of 5** and a progress bar.
- **Two fields to start**: Business asks only for a business name and a website.
- **Leave any time**: **Save and exit** is on every step.

## Start with your business

Enter your business name and website, then click **Continue**. Affitor creates the program and opens Commission for it.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/brand-onboarding-flow/business.png" alt="Step 1 of 5, Your business, with Business name, Website and Continue" />

## Keep going, or come back later

Commission, Tracking and Payments each have **Back** and **Continue**. On Tracking and Payments, **Continue** works while Affitor is still checking; the step is not marked done until the check passes. Go live still checks that everything is in place before your program goes live.

**Save and exit** saves your draft and opens its dashboard. With nothing typed on Business, it takes you back without creating a program.

## Where to change it later

Once a program is live, it has no setup: each step is its page in Program settings, and old setup links open that page.

## Conclusion

Setting up a program is now one guided flow from Business to Go live. [Learn more by creating a program](https://affitor.com/brand/new), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
