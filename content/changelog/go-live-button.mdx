Your program goes live when you say so. A single **Go Live** button on the Share Program step replaces automatic publishing, and it needs only your business profile and commission. Since October 2026, a program paused for an overdue invoice goes live again only after the invoice is paid.

The Quick start checklist in the top bar now reflects what is really set up: business profile, commission, tracking, a verified first real event, and going live.
