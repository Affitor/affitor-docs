### Improved

- When your account has nothing to connect yet, the AI app consent screen offers Join as partner or Create program, then takes you back to approve.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/improvements-2026-11-06/consent-empty.png" alt="The consent page with Nothing to connect yet and two choices, Join as partner and Create program" />

### Fixed

- Skip for now on payout setup applies only to the account you are signed in with. Another account on the same browser keeps its own Finish setup.

### For developers

- Install the [Affitor plugin](https://github.com/Affitor/claude-plugins) in Claude Code, or the extension in Gemini CLI, and sign in with your Affitor account. Access is read-only and needs no API key.
