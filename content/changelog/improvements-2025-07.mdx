### New

- Referral links get a short link you can share.
- Sign in to the Affitor community forum with your Affitor account.
- Free accounts see their daily allowance on the profile page.
- In the script assistant, save your own prompts and reuse them.

### Improved

- Program pages are redesigned, and the program list gets a new filter bar with country and launch year filters.
- The pricing page opens on yearly billing.

### Fixed

- Sorting by recurring commission works correctly.

The program list became the Affitor Marketplace in June 2026, and the script assistant is no longer part of Affitor.
