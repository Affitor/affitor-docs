Affitor has its own affiliate program. Find the **Refer Program** in the marketplace, read the official terms on its landing page, and share your link. You earn $50 when a brand you referred goes live, $350 when its program reaches $10,000 in tracked sales, and 15% of the platform fee it pays for 12 months.
