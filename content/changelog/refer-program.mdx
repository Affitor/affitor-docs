Affitor has its own affiliate program. Find the **Refer Program** in the marketplace, read the official terms on its landing page, and share your link. You earn a recurring share of the platform fee from every brand you refer, and your **Referred Brands** list shows who joined.

Brands also get a program handle: a stable public address for the program, with old links redirecting to it.

[Read the Refer Program terms](/docs/partners/refer-program)
