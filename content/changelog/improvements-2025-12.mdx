### New

- A light and dark theme switch.
- One account can switch between brand and partner roles.
- Accept, mute or delete a conversation.
- Brand onboarding ends with a setup completion step.

### Improved

- **Program details are redesigned**, with draft saving and image preview.
- Partner and application lists have search, sorting and page numbers.
- Test events no longer count in your performance numbers.
- Disconnecting Stripe takes effect right away.
- Clearer labels for conversions, sign-ups and commission status.
