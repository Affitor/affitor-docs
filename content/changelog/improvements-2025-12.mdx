### New

- One account can switch between brand and partner roles.

### Improved

- **The public program page is redesigned.** In program settings, you can save a draft of your changes in this browser and preview your logo before saving.
- Partner and application lists have search, sorting and page numbers.
- Test events no longer count in your performance numbers.
- Disconnecting Stripe takes effect right away.
- Clearer labels for paid sign-ups and for commissions waiting for payout.
