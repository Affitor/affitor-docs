### Improved

- **Chat** groups messages under a centered day and time at each new day or after an hour of quiet. Times show on hover or tap; only brands see writer names.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/improvements-2026-11-10/chat-dividers.png" alt="A partner chat on the brand side: centered Today dividers with their times, the partner's name above each run of her messages, a teammate's name above their run on the right, and one message's time shown under it on hover" />

- You type a new password once, in one **New password** field, on **Login and security** in your account and on the **Set a new password** page.
- The **Planned** integrations on **Integrations** show their logos: Dodo Payments, Paddle, Vercel and Stripe App. Webhooks has an icon.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/improvements-2026-11-10/planned.png" alt="The Planned section of the Integrations page: Dodo Payments and Paddle under Payments, Vercel and Stripe App under App platforms, Webhooks under Automation, each tile with its logo (an icon for Webhooks) and a Planned tag" />

- **Auto-approve** is a **Yes** (Link right away) or **No** (You approve first) choice in program setup and in Program settings › **Status**. Saved choices stay as they are.
- On **Members**, the permission level menu that Owners and Admins use describes **Member** as "Partners and the program, no money".
- The docs have a new [Team roles](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/team-roles) page: what Owners, Admins, Members and Viewers can do in a program.
- Connecting an AI app, including reconnecting the same one, always opens the consent screen for the account you are signed in with.
- AI apps that list commissions over MCP (`list_commissions`, `list_my_commissions`) get each commission's `display_status`, and a `held_reason` when it is on hold or not eligible.
- The **Muse Code** steps in **Connect an AI tool** use Muse Code's canonical `settings.json` spelling: `mcpServers` with `type: "streamable-http"`. A file with both `mcpServers` and `mcp_servers` disables MCP.
- Visits through a full `?via=` referral link keep their referral credit but no longer add to **Clicks** under **From the old referral program** on **Referrals**. Short links still record clicks.
- We tightened the address checks Affitor applies when it reads a program's website for its logo and health check.

### Fixed

- On **Partners**, a partner's **Commission** now counts a commission partly refunded after approval on a $0 sale, as do the earnings AI apps read. Balances and payouts are unchanged.
- Switching a group that still pays per click or per lead to Cost Per Sale in **Edit Group** starts with an empty **Commission Rate (%)**, required before saving.
- On the **Next.js + Clerk**, **Next.js + NextAuth** and **Next.js + Supabase** integration pages, **Read the guide** opens the Next.js guide on that library's tab, not **No auth library**.
- On a partner's **Programs** list, a joined program that its brand paused shows **Paused**, not **Active**.
- A sign-up with a password keeps **Check Your Email** on screen after **Create account**, with the address you entered; sign-ups by email code or Google never show it.
- A password chosen at sign-up stops working when you confirm by the email link: sign in with **Email me a code** or set a new password. Confirmed accounts are unchanged.
