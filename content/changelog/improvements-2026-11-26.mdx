### New

- Affitor is now in the [ChatGPT app directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ae91052cc5c8191bbe5935e898cbeb4): install it and sign in with your Affitor account (brands pick the programs to share), and ChatGPT can read your data.

### Improved

- ChatGPT connections are now read-only, including ones made earlier with **Full access**: the Allow screen offers **Read account data**, and ChatGPT can't publish, review applications or change terms.
- The marketplace's default list now shows only programs at the [Launch level](https://affitor.com/docs/brand/marketplace-levels); programs we emailed about this keep their place until the date in that email.
- A live program below Launch shows under **All programs** with a **Pre-launch** label, and joins the default list on its own once it meets every condition.
- In the **Roles** tab of **Members**, each role's Chat line now has a switch that puts Admins, Members or Viewers in every chat with partners; Owners are always in.
- Text boxes that aren't for signing in, such as search and the invite email, now ask password managers to leave them alone.
- Opening a menu, a select or a dialog in the dashboard keeps the page still instead of shifting it sideways.
- `@affitor/sdk` 2.1.5: `trackSale` and `trackRefund` retry a rate-limited (429) answer up to 3 times, waiting as `Retry-After` says; `maxRetries: 0` turns this off.
- We tightened how dashboard requests reach our servers, including our internal admin tools. Nothing changes for you.
