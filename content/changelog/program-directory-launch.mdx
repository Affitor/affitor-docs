Affitor starts here: one place to find affiliate programs worth promoting. Search by name, sort by commission or monthly traffic, filter by category and tag, and open a page for each program with its commission details.

- Program pages show popular YouTube and TikTok videos about the program, its estimated monthly web traffic and its top search keywords.
- Works on phones, with search and navigation sized for small screens.
- Light and dark theme.

The directory became the Affitor Marketplace in June 2026, and old directory pages now open it. [Browse the marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace)
