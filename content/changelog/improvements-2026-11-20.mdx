### Improved

- The brand dashboard shows its numbers sooner, most of all on a first visit: it now reads each piece of data once.
- The marketplace opens faster after a quiet spell: you see the saved program list right away while a fresh copy loads.
- Public pages on affitor.com load less code: Google Analytics now loads once instead of twice.
- On the partner Programs page, the loading placeholder has the same shape as the page, so the page no longer jumps when it finishes loading.
- In the docs, **Brands > Track sales** now links the Polar guide and the server-to-server guide for other payment gateways, such as SePay.
- Docs pages load faster: their fonts come from the docs site itself, and a survey script we don't use no longer downloads.
- We tightened sign-in to our internal admin tools. Nothing changes for you.

### Fixed

- Signing in with Google works again for accounts whose Google profile name or photo link is very long.
