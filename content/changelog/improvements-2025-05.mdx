### New

- **Top Ads.** See ads running for affiliate offers, read their transcripts, and send one to the script assistant.

### Improved

- Google sign-in now works from links opened inside apps such as Facebook, Instagram and X: we open the page in your regular browser, and Safari is no longer blocked.
- Lists show a loading outline instead of a blank page.

### Fixed

- The same ad no longer appears twice in Top Ads.

Top Ads and the script assistant are no longer part of Affitor.
