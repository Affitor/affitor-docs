---
title: Connect Stripe and see every commission
date: 2025-10-16
type: feature
audience: [brands, partners]
surfaces: [dashboard, tracking]
description: Brands connect their Stripe account so sales are matched to partners, and a new Commissions screen lists every commission with filters.
---

Brands can connect their Stripe account to Affitor. Payments made through it are matched to the partner who referred the customer, and commissions are created for you.

The new **Commissions** screen lists every commission with filters. Brands see it per program; partners see their own across programs.
