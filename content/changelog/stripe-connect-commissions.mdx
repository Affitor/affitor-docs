---
title: Connect Stripe and see every commission
date: 2025-10-29
type: feature
audience: [brands, partners]
surfaces: [dashboard, tracking]
description: Brands connect their Stripe account so checkout sales are matched to partners, and a new Commissions screen lists each program's commissions with filters.
---

Brands can connect their Stripe account to Affitor. When a referred customer pays through the Affitor checkout on your connected account, the sale is matched to the partner who referred them and the commission is created for you. We later retired that checkout.

The new **Commissions** screen lists each program's commissions with filters. Partners see the commissions they earned in each program they joined.
