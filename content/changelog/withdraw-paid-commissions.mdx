Starting with commissions approved on Oct 9, a partner can withdraw a commission once the brand has paid the invoice that includes it. Commissions approved before that date are not affected.

## For partners

Your payout page shows a new line under **Ready to withdraw**: **Awaiting brand payment**. It shows the total of approved commissions you can withdraw as soon as the brand pays its weekly invoice. When the brand pays, that amount moves into your available balance on its own, and you withdraw it as usual.

Your approved commissions still count toward your earnings right away. Only the timing of the withdrawal changes.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/withdraw-paid-commissions/payout.png" alt="The partner payout page: Ready to withdraw, with Awaiting brand payment underneath" />

## For brands

You no longer mark commissions as paid. Paying your weekly invoice is what releases your partners' commissions, so the **Mark as Paid** action is gone from the Commissions page.

If you want your partners paid sooner, pay the invoice as soon as it arrives. Billing shows what is due and when.
