Pricing is now public and simple. Every program is free until its affiliate sales pass $10,000 in total. Beyond that, the platform fee is 3.5% of each affiliate sale: a $100 sale costs $3.50. Partner commissions are paid as you set them; the fee is separate.

[See pricing](/pricing) or read [how the fee works](/docs/getting-started/pricing-performance-model).
