### New

- **See what people say about a program.** Program pages show posts about it from Reddit and X, sorted by views or comments, with load more.
- Program pages show estimated monthly web traffic and the top search keywords.
- Sign in with your Google account.

### Improved

- The home page and search work on phones.
- Programs are sorted by popularity by default.
