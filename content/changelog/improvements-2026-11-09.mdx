### New

- **Integrations** has a **Planned** section under **All**: Dodo Payments, Paddle, Vercel, Stripe App and Webhooks.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/improvements-2026-11-09/planned.png" alt="The Planned section on Integrations: Dodo Payments and Paddle under Payments, Vercel and Stripe App under App platforms, Webhooks under Automation, each with a Planned pill" />

### Improved

- **Stripe Payment Links** on **Integrations** is marked **One-click**: one approval on Stripe's page, with no key and no webhook.
- The guide links on the Clerk, Supabase and NextAuth integration pages open the signup step of the Next.js guide.
- A missing page shows **Page not found** and one button: **Go to Dashboard** when signed in, **Go to Marketplace** when not, with links to Docs, Pricing and Integrations.
- The homepage steps and FAQ describe revenue share on each sale: one-time, recurring for a set number of months, or lifetime.
- The footer on the homepage, Pricing, Docs, Blog and the Changelog links to Affitor on GitHub.

### Fixed

- Partners who open a link to **Integrations** in their dashboard while signed out see it right after signing in, without reloading.
- Editing a group that still pays per click or per lead keeps those terms unless you pick new ones.
