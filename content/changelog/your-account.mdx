Open [Your account](https://affitor.com/account) for everything about you, on either side of Affitor.

Until this week, your profile and payout settings lived on separate pages for brands and partners. Now there is one page for the person, with three parts: **Profile**, **Login and security** and **Payouts**.

## How it works

- **One menu**: click your name at the bottom of the rail.
- **One page**: the gear in that menu opens Your account, whichever side you are on.
- **Old links still work**: earlier profile links open Your account.

## Open the account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the left rail. The menu switches you between the brand and partner sides, and has **Home page**, **Docs** and **Log out**. The gear opens **Your account**.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/your-account/menu.png" alt="The account menu open from the profile row, with a gear for Your account, Switch to partner, Refer a brand, Home page, Docs and Log out" />

## Profile

Your name, email, username and country sit in one card. Click your avatar to pick a photo, then drag and zoom it in **Adjust your avatar** before you save.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/your-account/profile.png" alt="Your account on the Profile tab, with the avatar and the name, email, username and country fields" />

## Login and security

Change your password here, and use **Sign out other devices** to end every other browser, phone and CLI session. This device stays signed in.

## Payouts

Partners get ready to be paid here: a legal name, a payout method and a tax form. See [Get paid through Wise or a bank transfer](https://affitor.com/changelog/partner-payouts-wise).

## Back to your side

**Back** at the top of Your account returns you to the side you came from. **Home page** in the menu opens the Affitor home page, and when you are signed in, opening affitor.com takes you back to the side you used last.

## Conclusion

Your account is now one page, a click or two from anywhere in the dashboard. [Learn more about account settings](https://affitor.com/docs/partners/account-settings), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
