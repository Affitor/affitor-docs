See what a referral can earn and get your link on the new [Refer Program page](https://affitor.com/marketplace/referral-program).

Until today, the page listed the three rewards, a link and the terms. The $50 came when a referred brand's program went live, the $350 at $10,000 in tracked sales, and both reached your balance when their hold ended. Now the page shows a worked example, and the terms tie both milestones to paid invoices: each one is reviewed against those payments before it reaches your balance.

## How it works

- **Example**: set how many brands you bring and their monthly partner sales to see the three rewards add up.
- **Paid milestones**: $50 after the brand pays its first fee invoice; $350 at $10k in paid sales, with paid Affitor invoices and paid partners.
- **Reviewed release**: after the 30-day hold, the $50 and $350 are reviewed one by one. The 15% releases on its own.

## See what a referral could earn

The **Example** card starts at 3 brands and $25k in partner sales a month. Change either one and each reward line updates. Below it, the page explains how it works, why to refer, who it is for, and answers common questions.

## Get your link

Click **Get your link**. Signed in, it opens **Referrals**; otherwise you sign up first. Referrals shows your link with **Copy** and **Invite via email**, the same three rewards, and a note that the milestones are approved after the hold.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/refer-program-page/link.png" alt="Referrals with the three reward lines, the approval note and your link with Copy" />

## Where to read the rules

The [Refer Program terms](https://affitor.com/legal/refer-program) are the rules. **Refer a partner** shows **Soon** until its terms are published.

## Conclusion

The Refer Program page now shows what a referral can earn, and its milestones are reviewed against paid invoices before release. [Learn more about referral rewards](https://affitor.com/docs/partners/referral-rewards), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
