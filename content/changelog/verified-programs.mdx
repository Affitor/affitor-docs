Find programs Affitor has verified on the [marketplace](https://affitor.com/marketplace?verified=true).

Until this week, every listed program looked the same. Now a blue check after a program's name means Affitor checked its tracking, payments and invoices.

## How it works

- **Automatic**: Affitor checks every listed program; nobody applies.
- **Rechecked**: a program keeps its check while its tracking, payments and invoices stay in order.
- **Not a review**: the check is about how the program runs on Affitor, not about the product.

## Show verified programs only

Open **Filter** and choose **Verified only**. The list and Featured narrow to verified programs. The **Verified** chip shows the filter is on; **Clear** removes it.

## See what the check means

Hover or tap a check to see what Affitor checked.

<Media src="/docs/changelog/verified-programs/check.png" alt="A program card with the blue check and its explanation" />

## For brands

There is nothing to apply for. Run your tracking, payments and invoices through Affitor, and your program keeps its check.

## Conclusion

Partners can now pick programs whose tracking, payments and invoices Affitor has checked. [Learn more about finding programs](https://affitor.com/docs/partners/quickstart/find-and-join-programs), and write to support@affitor.com with any question.
